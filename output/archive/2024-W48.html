Introducing the Model Context Protocol

Anthropic has open-sourced the Model Context Protocol (MCP), a standardized framework designed to link AI assistants directly with external data sources like repositories and business tools. This protocol aims to enhance the accuracy and relevance of AI model responses by providing seamless access to siloed information.
