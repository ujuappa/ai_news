Gemini Robotics 2 brings whole body intelligence to robots
Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2, an intelligence layer powering adaptable robots with whole-body control, fine dexterity, and teamwork. This suite includes Gemini Robotics 2 (a VLA model for motor control), Gemini Robotics ER 2 (an embodied reasoning model for planning multi-step tasks and human communication), and Gemini Robotics On-Device 2 (an efficient VLA model for local deployment and rapid adaptation to new robot bodies). Gemini Robotics ER 2 is available via Google AI Studio, with VLA and On-Device models for early-access partners.

Gemini Robotics ER 2: powering robotics with video understanding, task orchestration, and multi-robot collaboration
Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics ER 2, an "embodied reasoning" model designed to serve as a high-level brain for robots. This version allows robots to understand video feeds to track progress, adapt to changes, and orchestrate multi-step tasks faster than ER 1.6 by thinking and acting simultaneously. It also introduces multi-robot collaboration, enabling robots to work together on complex tasks.
