Department of Commerce Announces Letters of Intent With 7 Companies for $874 Million to Accelerate Semiconductor R&D for the Compute Supply Chain
The Department of Commerce announced $874 million in federal incentives under the CHIPS and Science Act, distributed through letters of intent with 7 companies. These funds aim to accelerate semiconductor R&D for the compute supply chain, fostering advanced computing and AI systems for various industries. The investments will support technologies like integrated photonics, compute architectures, advanced packaging, and memory to boost U.S. leadership in semiconductors.

EU to Invest $11.4 Billion in 7 AI Gigafactories
The European Union is investing $11.4 billion to establish seven AI gigafactories, aiming to boost its AI computing power and reduce reliance on foreign providers. These facilities are planned to house at least 100,000 cutting-edge AI chips each, quadrupling the EU's current data center capacity. This initiative seeks to attract an additional $22.8 billion in private investment to help Europe compete with the U.S. and China in AI development.
