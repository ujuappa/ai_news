Policy & business SpaceX to Remove xAI Turbines by July 2027

SpaceX confirmed that it will take until July 2027 to remove the unpermitted gas turbines powering xAI's data centers, currently operating 69 units, as it transitions to a permanent 1.2 gigawatt natural gas plant. This comes amidst lawsuits from the NAACP and Southern Environmental Law Center over the unpermitted operations, with SpaceX planning to invest $2.8 billion in gas turbines over the next three years. The Department of Justice sided with SpaceX, citing national security concerns for the continued operation of the turbines in a highly polluted region south of Memphis.
