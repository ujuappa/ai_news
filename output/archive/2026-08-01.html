AI Disproves 87-Year-Old Jacobian Conjecture

An Anthropic mathematician, Levent Alpöge, used the Claude Fable 5 large language model to find a counterexample disproving the Jacobian conjecture for dimensions above two. This 87-year-old mathematical problem, previously a subject of many failed proofs, remains open only for its original two-dimensional form. This discovery highlights the growing role of AI in aiding complex mathematical research.
