California AI Disclosure Rules Become Operative August 2, 2026
California's AI Transparency Act (CAITA) has become operative as of August 2, 2026, imposing disclosure and detection requirements on generative AI providers with over one million monthly users in California. The initial phase mandates visible and machine-readable disclosures for AI-generated images, video, and audio, along with free public AI detection tools. Further requirements for hosting platforms and device manufacturers will be rolled out in 2027 and 2028, with noncompliance carrying civil penalties.
