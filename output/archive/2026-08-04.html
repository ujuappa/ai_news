Policy & business EU AI Act & California AI Transparency Act Converge

Both the EU AI Act and California AI Transparency Act came into force on August 2, 2026, establishing converging global transparency obligations for AI-generated content. These laws mandate that synthetic media, such as deepfakes, must be identifiable and traceable, with providers required to embed machine-readable markers and, in some cases, provide public detection tools. The EU Act imposes penalties up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, while California's law sets a $5,000 per violation fine.

Tools & products Skunk Works Advances Sensor-Powered AI Fighter Intercept

Lockheed Martin Skunk Works and the U.S. Air Force Test Pilot School demonstrated sensor-driven AI autonomy on an X-62 VISTA fighter jet. Across eight flights, an AI agent used real-time sensor data from a Legion Pod to execute 27 successful air intercepts against a live T-38 jet target. This project validated closing the sensor-to-action loop with AI in an operational combat aircraft and accelerated autonomy development, with full integration and ground testing occurring in just three months.

Policy & business Open-weight AI Models Catching Up, Safety Gap Remains

A report by SaferAI indicates that China's open-weight model, GLM-5.2 from Z.ai, is rapidly closing the capability gap with frontier models like OpenAI's GPT-5.5 and Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.7. However, GLM-5.2 showed no refusals on offensive cyber or dual-use biology tasks during evaluation, highlighting a significant safety gap compared to models with more robust safeguards. This raises concerns about the risks associated with highly capable open-weight models lacking sufficient safety measures once released.

Policy & business Nvidia's Open Secure AI Alliance Shows Rapid Progress

The Open Secure AI Alliance (OSAA), led by Nvidia and now comprising over 120 companies, has quickly made progress by forming the Shared AI Findings Exchange (SAFE) working group. This group, less than two weeks old, is already proposing guidelines for reporting AI cybersecurity incidents and sharing open-source technologies for securing AI agents.
