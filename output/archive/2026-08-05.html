DiffusionGemma Technical Report: Fast Text Generation

DiffusionGemma is an experimental open-weight language model using discrete diffusion for exceptionally fast text generation. It refines blocks of 256 tokens in parallel, bypassing the sequential decoding bottleneck of autoregressive LLMs. Fine-tuned from a Gemma 4 mixture-of-experts model with 3.8B activated and 25.2B total parameters, it uses less than 10% of the original model's training token budget.
