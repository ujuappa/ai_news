AMD and HPE Expand Collaboration on "Helios" AI Platform

AMD and HPE are expanding their collaboration to advance open, scalable AI infrastructure. HPE will be among the first to adopt AMD's "Helios" rack-scale AI architecture, which integrates AMD EPYC CPUs, Instinct GPUs, Pensando networking, and the ROCm open software stack. This platform aims to simplify large-scale AI cluster deployments and will be used to power "Herder," a supercomputer with AMD Instinct MI430X GPUs and next-gen AMD EPYC "Venice" CPUs, achieving up to 2.9 exaFLOPS of FP4 performance per rack.
