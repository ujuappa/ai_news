Firebird Opens Region's Largest AI Factory, Expands Globally
Firebird has launched what it claims is the largest NVIDIA DSX AI factory in the region, located in Armenia, with plans to expand across emerging markets. NVIDIA intends to invest in Firebird, following an earlier investment by CoreWeave, to support the deployment of over 70,000 NVIDIA Rubin and Blackwell GPUs by late 2027. This initiative aims to establish Armenia as a strategic AI computing hub, with Firebird's global AI infrastructure pipeline projected to reach 2 gigawatts by 2028 across Armenia, Kazakhstan, and other frontier markets.

California Lawmakers Target AI 'Therapist' Limits
California lawmakers are attempting to regulate AI chatbots offering mental health advice through Senate Bill 903. The proposed bill would prohibit advertising chatbots as therapy, ban AI from making therapeutic decisions without professional review, and require patient consent for AI use in recording sessions or triage. This move comes amid growing concerns over the accuracy and safety of AI in mental healthcare, with some lawsuits alleging AI chatbots contributed to user suicides.
