Embattled hedge fund Situational Awareness invests $400M in chip…

Situational Awareness, a hedge fund, invested $400 million in Source Foundry, a chip startup, bringing its total investment to $500 million despite recent significant losses in its public portfolio. The fund, founded by former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner, had previously sold off most of its public holdings to Citadel but retained its Anthropic shares. Source Foundry aims to revolutionize chip manufacturing, making it faster and cheaper.

Discovered Materials is playing AI whack-a-mole to hunt cooler chips

Discovered Materials, a startup, raised $9 million in seed funding to use AI agents to find new materials for cooler computer chips. The company, which emerged from Y Combinator, employs Anthropic models and foundational physics models to simulate and identify materials that can improve chip efficiency. They aim to patent and license these discoveries to major chipmakers, addressing the significant heat and power consumption issues in AI data centers.
