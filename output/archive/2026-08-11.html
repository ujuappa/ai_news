California Announces AI Cyber Defense Program

California Governor Gavin Newsom has announced a pioneering AI Cyber Defense Program to safeguard the state's critical infrastructure from AI-enabled cyber threats. This initiative builds on previous executive orders and directs state agencies to establish an AI Cyber Defense Program, enhance cybersecurity coordination, and expand AI-enabled defenses for local governments. Each state agency will also designate an AI Cybersecurity Officer to ensure preparedness.

IBM, Together AI Ink $240 Million Agreement to Scale AI

IBM and Together AI have entered a multi-year agreement worth $240 million to construct a large-scale AI cluster using Nvidia HGX B300 systems on IBM Cloud. This partnership aims to expand AI deployments, particularly for open-source model inference, and is expected to be operational by Q1 2027. The collaboration will also help Together AI penetrate the enterprise market and enhance accessibility to open-source AI.
