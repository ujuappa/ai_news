California Announces AI Cyber Defense Program

California Governor Gavin Newsom has announced a pioneering AI Cyber Defense Program to safeguard the state's critical infrastructure from AI-enabled cyber threats. This initiative builds on previous executive orders and directs state agencies to establish an AI Cyber Defense Program, enhance cybersecurity coordination, and expand AI-enabled defenses for local governments. Each state agency will also designate an AI Cybersecurity Officer to ensure preparedness.

Model releases Meta’s New Glimmer AI Model Offers Hint at Zuckerberg’s Vision

Meta released Muse Glimmer, an open-weight, 30-billion parameter AI model designed to power personal AI agents locally on consumer hardware. Glimmer, an open version of Meta's Muse Spark, supports multi-step tasks, text, and images across over 100 languages, aiming for privacy-sensitive, always-on operation without internet connectivity. This release provides a tangible example of Mark Zuckerberg's vision for widely distributed "personal superintelligence."

Model releases Motif 3: A 314B Parameter Mixture-of-Experts Model

Motif 3 is a new decoder-only Mixture-of-Experts language model featuring 314 billion total parameters, with 13.2 billion activated per token and 384 routed experts per layer, selecting eight per token. It incorporates Grouped Differential Latent Attention (GDLA), modified manifold-constrained hyper-connections, Expert Specific PolyNorm activations, and multi-token prediction to enhance optimization, specialization, and inference, pretrained on 12.5 trillion tokens.

Model releases Macaron-V1: Open Continual Learning with Self-Improvement

Macaron-V1 is an open agent-model family designed for continual learning from real-world experience, featuring recursive model-harness improvement and a Mixture-of-LoRA (MoL) architecture. The flagship Macaron-V1-Venti combines a 744B GLM-5.2 base with four specialist LoRAs for chat, agent, coding, and GenUI, while Macaron-V1-Tall (50B) uses a Qwen3.6 base for local deployment.
