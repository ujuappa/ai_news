Research Unreleased Anthropic model makes progress on Riemann hypothesis

An unreleased Anthropic model made significant progress on the Riemann hypothesis, one of mathematics' biggest unsolved problems, by increasing the lower bound of known solutions. The model, prompted by a staff member without extensive math training, explored 650 ideas and coordinated 60 subagents over 1.5 days, spending 31 million output tokens, highlighting AI's growing potential for scientific discovery.

Research Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs

Researchers have identified an architectural vulnerability allowing the stealing of reasoning traces from proprietary LLM APIs. By injecting an encrypted reasoning trace from a stronger model into a weaker, less safeguarded model from the same provider, they can force the weaker model to decode and output the trace. This exploit leverages the full compatibility and interchangeability of encrypted blocks across sessions, users, and models within a provider's ecosystem.
