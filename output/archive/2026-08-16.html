GLM-5.3: Z.ai Coding Model, Benchmarks & Weights

Z.ai released GLM-5.3, an open-weight 743B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model that significantly improves coding and agentic performance over GLM-5.2 through scaled-up post-training rather than architectural changes. The model demonstrates a 6x jump on Terminal-Bench 3.0 (4.6 to 28.3) and a ~50% improvement on Z.ai's internal Code Bench, even developing an unplanned cybersecurity skill. It achieves 31.4% on Code Bench with ~50,000 output tokens per task, outperforming Claude Opus 4.8's 29.5% with 120,000 tokens.

Wynd Kaufman, a 69-year-old activist, has become the first person jailed for protesting against artificial intelligence after chaining OpenAI's headquarters. Dubbed the "Rosa Parks of AI risk," Kaufman's action with the group StopAI highlighted concerns about the pursuit of artificial superintelligence. Her jailing underscores the growing tension and activism surrounding AI's development and societal impact.

Research BDH-CQ: In-Context Learning with Recurrent Latent Reasoning

BDH-CQ is a new reasoning model combining in-context learning with recurrent latent reasoning, which continuously updates its memory at inference. It solves queries through iterative computation in a high-dimensional latent space without verbalizing intermediate steps. A 150M-parameter version achieved 29.5% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1 at a cost of $0.0007 per task, surpassing previous cost-accuracy frontiers.

Policy & business Scam Uses AI to Exploit Holiday Photos for Bank Details

A scam is exploiting holiday photos posted on social media, using AI to generate personalized emails that seek bank account details. Fraudsters leverage details like travel locations to create convincing messages, such as a text about "unusual activity" while traveling in Porto. This sophisticated scam highlights the growing privacy and security risks associated with sharing personal information online and the use of AI in fraud.

Policy & business Anthropic Details Claude Watermarking for EU AI Act

Anthropic has provided more details on how Claude's new watermarks will function, following user debate and compliance with the EU AI Act. The SynthID-Text approach embeds undetectable patterns in generated text, without affecting quality, and a detection API will be released. Light editing won't remove watermarks completely, but complete rewrites will, and code will have less watermarking where choices are constrained.
