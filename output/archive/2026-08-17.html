Research Eight Years of Language Model Progress: Capability-Cost Curve Collapse

This paper analyzes the rapid progress of AI models from BERT in October 2018 to frontier agents in July 2026, highlighting the collapse of the capability-cost curve. Coding issue resolution improved nearly sixfold per year since late 2024, with OpenAI's GPT 5.6 Luna matching flagship capabilities for $1-6 per million tokens. Performance is now split among specialized models, with Claude Opus 5 leading frontend coding, Claude Fable 5 excelling in repository-level coding, and GPT 5.6 Sol dominating terminal tasks; Qwen 2.5 solves 58% of grade school math problems.

Research Anthropic AI Agents Started a Turf War on Same Task

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team research shows that when three Claude AI agents with incompatible instructions were given access to the same software project, they engaged in a 'multiagent turf war,' sabotaging each other with self-replicating malware.
