Research ASI-Bench: New Benchmark for Artificial Superintelligence

ASI-Bench is introduced as the first benchmark to evaluate AI systems' capabilities for innovative exploration and autonomous scientific execution across general research domains. It aims to assess AI's ability to create new knowledge and verify results, moving beyond current benchmarks that primarily test existing knowledge application. The benchmark progressively withdraws human meta-guidance to push AI towards artificial superintelligence.

Policy & business OpenAI institutes new safeguards after Hugging Face breach

OpenAI has instituted new safeguards for its AI models following the Hugging Face breach, focusing on enhanced monitoring, alignment, and security during development and post-training. The company paused reinforcement learning (RL) for two weeks and is implementing stricter network isolation and monitoring systems. These measures are driven by concerns about model capabilities and the pace of AI progress, with alerts aimed for within 30 minutes of suspicious activity.

Research Claude accelerates protein design and analytical chemistry

Anthropic's Claude model (Mythos Preview and Opus 4.8) has demonstrated significant capability in accelerating protein design and analytical chemistry. It successfully designed protein binders against 14 out of 15 targets, with individual designs binding successfully between 22% and 35% of the time, outperforming the typical 10-15%. This can reduce a weeks-long process to a fraction of the time, with some designs binding several times more tightly than previous best results.

Tools & products Palo Alto Networks Introduces Frontier AI Critical Defense

Palo Alto Networks launched the Frontier AI Critical Defense Program to protect critical infrastructure from AI-driven cyber exploits. The program collaborates with leading technology providers like IBM, Microsoft, Anthropic, and OpenAI to deploy proactive "virtual patches" at the network level. This aims to neutralize vulnerabilities identified by frontier AI models, such as the 14,000 previously unknown flaws Palo Alto Networks uncovered, before attackers can exploit them.

Policy & business AI isn’t close to curing cancer. This startup says it knows

Biotech startup Vivodyne claims the AI drug-discovery industry has a data problem and has built robotic labs (HIVE) to generate crucial causal biological data. Vivodyne's HIVE can grow 20 kinds of human tissue and autonomously test them, producing data currently missing from AI models. The company recently opened a "human data center" and secured nearly $80 million in funding.
