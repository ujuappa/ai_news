Stripe buys OpenRouter for $7.5 billion

Stripe has acquired OpenRouter for an estimated $7.5 billion, significantly up from its May valuation of $1.3 billion, with founders receiving $1.5 billion. While the founders jokingly cited the "singularity" as a motive, the acquisition likely aims to embed Stripe further into AI capital flows, specifically for managing AI expenses. OpenRouter, known for routing prompts between AI models, will continue independent operations, aligning with Stripe's focus on developer platforms and its expanding role in the AI economy.

Policy & business OpenAI seeks to one-up Anthropic on privacy

OpenAI is introducing Private Safety Processing, a new privacy-centric safety approach that monitors for misuse across multiple conversations without retaining customer data. This system aims to detect sophisticated malicious AI use, like malware engineering, by analyzing inputs and outputs across sessions via an agent. This move appears to one-up Anthropic, whose recent data retention policy for "covered models" has concerned enterprise customers regarding sensitive data handling.

Policy & business Meet the startup helping Wall Street put a price on AI compute

Silicon Data has raised $30 million in Series A funding to establish a reference price for GPU rental and an index for Wall Street futures contracts. The company plans to launch compute futures trading on the CME on October 5th, pending regulatory approval. This initiative aims to address the lack of a straightforward pricing mechanism for AI compute, which has become the largest cost for AI product developers.

Tools & products Meta AI's new Mac app wants you to talk to your apps

Meta AI has launched a new Mac application that integrates system-wide dictation and screen context awareness using its Muse Spark model. This app allows users to interact with Meta AI through voice commands and get answers based on their current screen. Additionally, Meta AI is being updated for business owners to provide insights from connected Instagram, Facebook, and Google Workspace accounts, enabling tasks like drafting documents and analyzing campaign performance.

Tools & products Binance now lets AI agents trade

Binance has launched Agent OS, a platform allowing AI agents to analyze markets and execute trades for its over 300 million users. The platform integrates Binance APIs and other services, supporting AI tools like OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude Code. Binance emphasizes user control through sub-accounts and strict permissions to manage agent activity and mitigate risks, though the exchange has limited visibility into an agent's reasoning.

Tools & products Google packs Search and Gemini with new AI study tools

Google has launched new AI study tools across Search and Gemini, including AI-generated interactive visuals, 3D simulations, and customized practice quizzes. Students can now create study documents from uploaded files and engage in multi-step research reports with Gemini Live. These features, along with a dedicated student hub in the Gemini app, aim to position Gemini as a primary AI assistant for learning, competing with other AI education platforms.

Policy & business TerraPower's nuclear reactor has a secret weapon for AI data centers

Bill Gates-founded TerraPower plans to announce its first data center project this year, with a second plant breaking ground in 2027, following Meta's agreement to buy eight Natrium power plants. TerraPower's 345-megawatt molten salt-cooled reactor offers a key advantage: the ability to quickly ramp up and down power output without affecting the reactor itself, complementing intermittent renewable sources. This unique energy storage capability makes it ideal for powering AI data centers, whose fluctuating loads can stress traditional power grids.

Tools & products Amazon makes its AI-powered Alexa+ free on Fire TV

Amazon is making its AI assistant, Alexa+, available for free on all compatible Fire TV devices in the U.S., without requiring a Prime subscription. This update provides conversational search, smart home controls, and AI-powered recommendations to all users. The move reflects an industry-wide trend to integrate AI into consumer electronics, with Amazon reporting that Alexa+ users have nearly twice as many conversations on Fire TV as with the original Alexa.
