Starcloud Raises $250M for Orbital Data Centers, Valued at $2.3B

Starcloud, a startup focused on orbital data centers for AI inference, secured a $250 million extension to its Series A funding, valuing the company at $2.3 billion. This capital will fund a new manufacturing facility and advance their Starcloud-3 orbital data center spacecraft. The company is actively working to secure launch capacity, particularly with SpaceX's Starship, as the market for rocket transportation tightens up.

Policy & business AI Data Startup Micro1 Reaches $500M Gross Run Rate

AI data startup Micro1 has seen its gross annual run rate surge from $100 million to $500 million in the past eight months, driven by high demand for AI training data. The company, which provides data labeling services, retains 60% to 70% of this revenue. Micro1 also emphasizes that it does not sell its data to Chinese model makers, differentiating itself from some competitors amidst industry controversy.

Policy & business OpenAI Gaining on Anthropic with Business Users

New data from corporate credit card company Ramp indicates that OpenAI is starting to gain ground on Anthropic in market share among U.S. business users. While Anthropic currently holds a larger share at nearly 44% compared to OpenAI's nearly 40% as of July, OpenAI's growth rate in Q3 has been faster. This suggests that businesses are willing to switch between AI providers based on new model releases and specific use cases, indicating a dynamic and competitive market.

Research Inertia Enterprises Finds Fast Fusion Fuel Production Method

Fusion startup Inertia Enterprises has achieved a breakthrough in significantly reducing the time required to produce its fusion fuel pellets, from several days to just minutes. This advancement addresses a key hurdle in commercializing fusion technology, which had been a major premise for its $450 million in investor funding. The process, developed with help from the National Ignition Facility, can now create a single fuel pellet in two to three hours and can be scaled for industrial production.

Policy & business London Datacenter Would Emit 1M Tonnes CO2 Annually

A proposed hyperscale data center in North Ockendon, London, if approved, would generate over 1 million tonnes of carbon dioxide annually, equivalent to 27,000 flights from London to New York. Planning documents show that this facility, intended for AI and cloud computing, would use 218 hectares of green belt land. Environmental concerns highlight the conflict between technological expansion and UK net-zero targets.

Tools & products ChatGPT Can Now Send Texts via Apple Messages Plug-in

ChatGPT now offers a new Apple Messages plug-in, allowing users to connect their Messages inbox directly with the chatbot. This feature enables ChatGPT to sort, analyze, edit, draft, and send messages on behalf of the user, as well as search message history. OpenAI states the plug-in runs locally to address privacy concerns, but users are encouraged to monitor ChatGPT's actions, especially regarding sending messages.

Tools & products Google Offers Publishers New Way to Fight AI Traffic Losses

Google has introduced a new 'Preferred Sources' button for publishers, allowing readers to indicate favorite content sources to be highlighted across Google Search, Discover, and Google News. This initiative aims to combat AI-driven traffic losses for publishers by giving users more control over their news feeds. Google hopes this will direct more traffic to trusted sites, with studies showing users are twice as likely to click preferred sources.

Tools & products Is Vine Back? Divine Short-Form Video App Opens to Public

Divine, a new short-form video-sharing app, has launched as a spiritual successor to Vine, allowing users to create and share six-second looping videos. Notably, Divine bans all AI-generated content, requiring users to record videos live in the app and using verification tools like ProofMode to ensure human authorship. Backed by Twitter co-founder Jack Dorsey and built on a decentralized system called Nostr, the app aims to offer a "reset" for social media, prioritizing authenticity over AI content.
