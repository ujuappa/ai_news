Research Nvidia Shows Harness, Not AI Model, is Key for Long-Horizon Tasks

Nvidia's new research suggests that the software 'harness' surrounding an AI model is more crucial than the model itself for long-horizon tasks. By using a custom harness with strong memory handling and a supervisor component, Claude Opus 5 achieved a 100% score on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark, significantly outperforming its 30% score without the harness.

Tools & products Show HN: Huzzah – A Novel Approach to Coding with AI

Huzzah is an experimental editor offering a novel approach to coding with AI, where users write pseudocode that the editor then synchronizes to real source code upon saving. This aims to simplify interaction with coding agents, addressing the tedium of writing full sentences for every change and agents' complexity limits.
