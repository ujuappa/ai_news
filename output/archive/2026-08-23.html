Policy & business NVIDIA Acquires Poolside's Model Factory Technology for $600 Million

NVIDIA has reportedly acquired Poolside's "Model Factory" technology for $600 million, gaining a non-exclusive license to its Laguna series of open-source coding models. Over 100 Poolside engineers will join NVIDIA's Nemotron open-weight model project, which is rumored to be developing trillion-parameter models. This move signifies NVIDIA's strategic shift to deepen its presence in the model layer and compete directly in the open-source AI market, expanding beyond its core hardware business.
