Policy & business SpaceXAI Adopts NVIDIA Vera CPU to Accelerate Agentic AI

SpaceXAI, the AI division of SpaceX, is adopting NVIDIA Vera CPUs to enhance its agentic AI workloads, including those powering its Grok models. This move aims to accelerate AI agents by providing the necessary CPU performance for orchestration, code execution, and data processing, complementing GPU utilization. Additionally, SpaceXAI plans to extend this NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform into space with its first-generation Starmind AI satellite.

Policy & business OpenAI’s Jalapeño chip built for fast inference, benchmarks show

OpenAI has revealed more details about its custom AI chip, Jalapeño, at the Hot Chips conference, including benchmark results. The chip demonstrated significant performance advantages over current state-of-the-art inference processors, showing higher tokens per user and throughput per kilowatt on Semianalysis’s InferenceX benchmark. OpenAI expects small-volume deployment by the end of 2026, with significant deployment in 2027.

Policy & business Valor, Point72 back General Intuition at $6B valuation

General Intuition, an AI startup developing a foundation model for generalized AI agents in physical space, is reportedly in talks to raise funding at a $6 billion pre-money valuation. New investors like Valor Equity Partners and Point72 Ventures are expected to participate, with existing investors Khosla Ventures and General Catalyst also contributing. The funds will focus on improving the general model with an emphasis on robotic embodiments, requiring more compute infrastructure and talent.

Policy & business Accel-backed Keenable is indexing the web for AI agents

Accel-backed startup Keenable has emerged from stealth with $26 million in seed funding, aiming to build a web search index specifically for AI agents. Founded by former Yandex and Amazon search lead Andrey Styskin, the company is developing infrastructure optimized for AI's ability to process vast amounts of information, with an index of over 100 billion documents already in use by several AI labs.

Policy & business Taiwan charges 9 over illegal AI server exports to China

Taiwan has charged nine individuals, including staff from Nvidia and Super Micro, for illegally exporting AI servers to China. This action highlights the geopolitical sensitivities and regulatory efforts surrounding advanced technology transfers, especially concerning high-performance AI hardware.

Policy & business Instinct’s powerful AI assistant raises privacy concerns

Instinct, a powerful AI personal assistant, is generating buzz for its capabilities but also significant privacy and security concerns during its private testing phase. Users and testers have raised issues regarding its broad "perpetual and irrevocable" license to user data for model training, the collection of screen captures and keyboard inputs, and its ability to enter binding agreements. Despite the company addressing some initial issues like data deletion, concerns about its security model and data handling persist.

Australia's music industry has officially banned AI-generated songs from appearing on its music charts. This policy aims to address concerns about artistic integrity and fair competition in the music landscape as AI-powered music creation becomes more prevalent.
