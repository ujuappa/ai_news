EU Begins Enforcing Sweeping Artificial Intelligence Act

The European Union has begun enforcing key provisions of its Artificial Intelligence Act as of August 2, 2026, granting its AI Office and national authorities new powers. This includes regulating prohibited AI practices, general-purpose AI models, and mandating transparency for chatbots and AI-generated content. The law's reach extends extraterritorially, impacting American AI companies whose systems are used in the EU, potentially setting global standards due to compliance costs.

Policy & business Songs Created by AI Banned From Australia's Music Charts

Australia's ARIA has banned songs largely or wholly created by AI from its music charts, requiring all eligible releases to be "substantially human made." This policy, influenced by a controversy involving an AI-assisted cover that topped a chart, aims to promote human artistry and prevent the promotion of AI-generated music. While AI tools can still be used for tasks like mastering, human artists must be responsible for songwriting, lead vocals, and primary instrument performance, with declarations of AI use required upon submission.
