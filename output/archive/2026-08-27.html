Nvidia Closes In on Hugging Face Acquisition for $12.9B

Nvidia is reportedly nearing an acquisition of Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, a deal that would significantly bolster its position in open-source AI. The acquisition would give Nvidia a stronger foothold in the open-source community, providing more alternatives to closed AI systems and maintaining dependence on Nvidia's hardware. This move aligns with Nvidia's existing efforts to promote open-source AI and protect its dominance in AI chips.

Policy & business Amazon Triples Nvidia Chip Order for AWS Data Centers

Amazon has tripled its order of Nvidia GPUs, adding another 2 million Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra chips to its AWS data centers by 2027 and 2028. This expanded partnership, valued at tens of billions of dollars, includes integrating Nvidia's networking hardware, open models, and robotics platform across AWS. The deal reflects surging demand for AI compute and Nvidia's continued dominance, even as Amazon invests in its own AI chips.

Policy & business Nvidia Revenue Doubles to $96B on Continued AI Demand

Nvidia reported a massive increase in revenue, doubling to $96 billion in Q2 and projecting $108 billion for the next quarter, driven by strong AI demand. CEO Jensen Huang stated "AI has reached its inflection point," with the data center division alone generating $89 billion. Nvidia's financial success makes it the world's most valuable firm at over $5 trillion market cap and a key backer for AI companies like OpenAI and Anthropic.

Policy & business Anthropic Inks $45B Compute Deal with Nscale

Anthropic has signed a six-year, $45 billion deal with Nscale to secure AI compute, primarily using Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips, starting in late 2027. This follows other major compute partnerships, including $10 billion with Volta, $5 billion with AMD, and deals with SpaceX, Amazon, Google, and Broadcom. These agreements highlight Anthropic's aggressive strategy to scale compute capacity and compete in the AI landscape.

Policy & business Top Tech Firms Warn Time Running Out for Cyber Security

Over 100 tech firms, including Google, Microsoft, Anthropic, and OpenAI, signed an open letter warning that time is running out to beef up cyber defenses against increasingly sophisticated AI-powered attacks. The letter criticizes "historic under-resourcing" of security for critical infrastructure and calls for governments to provide "capable, defensive AI" and testing. This comes after recent cyber breaches, including OpenAI's AI agents hacking Hugging Face.
