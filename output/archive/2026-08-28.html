Open-Weight AI Companies are the Valley's Hottest Targets

Open-weight AI companies are becoming prime acquisition targets in Silicon Valley, highlighted by Nvidia's rumored $13 billion bid for Hugging Face and its $6 billion deal with Poolside, along with Stripe's $7 billion acquisition of OpenRouter. Policy & business Anthropic Wins First Court Case Against Pentagon Blacklisting

A federal judge ruled that the Trump administration's blacklisting of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk was illegal and arbitrary, violating the First and Fifth Amendments. The ruling stated that the government's action was retaliatory due to Anthropic's stance on safety guardrails for military AI use. This marks a significant legal victory for Anthropic against the Pentagon.

Policy & business OpenAI to Start Showing Ads on ChatGPT Free and Go Tiers in India

OpenAI will begin showing ads on ChatGPT's free and Go subscription tiers in India, following similar rollouts in the U.S. and Europe. The company has partnered with WPP and Omnicom for advertising and plans to launch an ad manager next month, with a minimum daily budget of ₹725 ($7.60). This move aims to ramp up revenue ahead of a potential IPO, with OpenAI reportedly generating $6.7 billion in Q2 2026.

Policy & business UN: All social media needs child safety reform after Meta deal

The UN human rights chief called for all social media to be designed to be safe for children, following Meta's $18 billion settlement with nearly all US states which includes new child safety measures. The UN and US officials are pushing for other platforms like TikTok and YouTube to adopt similar protections, emphasizing that governments should actively regulate rather than wait for companies or courts to act.
