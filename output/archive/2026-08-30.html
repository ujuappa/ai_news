Musk: SpaceX foundry to accelerate gas turbine production

Elon Musk has revealed that SpaceX is building a secret foundry in Bastrop, Texas, to produce natural gas turbine blades and vanes. This in-house casting aims to accelerate the deployment of natural gas turbines by up to 18 months, addressing a critical bottleneck in the energy supply for AI data centers. The global demand for power, projected to double by 2030 for data centers, has exhausted the capacity of the few companies capable of producing these specialized, heat-resistant blades.

Policy & business Sony Music, Warner sue Anthropic over IP theft claims

Sony Music Publishing, Warner Chappell, and other music publishers have filed a lawsuit against Anthropic, alleging a "brazen campaign" of intellectual property theft. The lawsuit claims Anthropic illegally torrented, scraped, and downloaded thousands of copyrighted musical works, including lyrics and sheet music, to train its Claude AI model. Anthropic stated it disagrees with the claims and intends to defend itself robustly, following previous similar IP lawsuits against the company.
