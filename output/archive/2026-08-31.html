AMD, Cisco, HUMAIN Launch Saudi AI Infrastructure

AMD, Cisco, and HUMAIN have launched AI infrastructure in Saudi Arabia, powered by AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs, AMD EPYC CPUs, and Cisco Silicon One networking. This collaboration aims to deploy up to 250 MW of AI infrastructure starting in 2027, with a long-term goal of 1 GW by 2030. The joint venture is providing GPU-as-a-service to customers in the region and globally.

Policy & business Nvidia's $3.5B MediaTek Bet Reveals AI Chip Strategy

Nvidia is investing $3.5 billion into MediaTek, a Taiwanese chipmaker, to integrate its NVLink Fusion technology for designing custom AI chips. This partnership aims to allow MediaTek to build custom silicon for AI companies and hyperscalers that can plug into Nvidia-based data centers, addressing the trend of major tech companies developing their own chips.
