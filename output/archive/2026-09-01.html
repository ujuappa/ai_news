Policy & business ChatGPT Health Adds Epic Integration for Clinicians

OpenAI's ChatGPT Health is integrating with Epic's electronic health record (EHR) system, allowing clinicians to import patient data and use AI for querying. This integration provides read-only access to health records for tasks like summarizing notes and reviewing patient history, but AI will not write back to records. OpenAI also introduced a Healthcare Public Data plugin and enables compliant workflows for organizations with a Business Associate Agreement, while emphasizing that AI is not for diagnosis or treatment, following prior legal issues regarding unsafe recommendations.

Policy & business Empirik Launches with $21M to Predict Outages

Sequoia-incubated Empirik has spun out as an independent company after raising $21 million in seed funding from Sequoia, Canapi, and Alumni Ventures. Empirik aims to predict and prevent tech outages using AI by tracking system changes and inferring their ripple effects across infrastructure. The startup claims to be a new type of observability tool, already serving Fortune 500 customers and automating routine troubleshooting for DevOps teams.

Policy & business AIR Raises $50M to Vet AI Agent Skills and Add-ons

AIR, an AI security startup, has emerged from stealth with $50 million in two seed rounds led by Sequoia and Greenoaks, respectively. The company provides a platform to discover AI agents, continuously vet their skills and add-ons, and block unapproved interactions with software or external sources. AIR aims to establish a security framework for the nascent AI agent software supply chain, akin to driver signing for operating systems.

Policy & business Apple Shares Evidence Against Ex-Employee in OpenAI Lawsuit

Apple has filed new evidence in its lawsuit against OpenAI, alleging that former Apple employee Chang Liu stole confidential data, including a circuit schematic and an internal engineering tool. Apple claims OpenAI was aware of Liu's access to this data and that Liu, with OpenAI colleague Yu-Ting Peng, attempted to destroy evidence. Apple is seeking a preliminary injunction and expedited discovery, asserting that over 400 former Apple employees now work at OpenAI, raising broader concerns about trade secret protection.

Policy & business Amazon Rigged $20 Billion Ad Prices, US Lawsuit Alleges

A US lawsuit by the FTC and 22 states alleges Amazon secretly overcharged over a million advertising customers by manipulating online auctions. The scheme is believed to have netted Amazon $20 billion since 2019, overriding actual auction results with higher prices. Amazon denies misleading advertisers and calls the suit misguided, stating that average winning bids for Sponsored Products search ads fell 50% from 2019 to 2025.

Tools & products Pentagon Launches ChatGPT Mil and Grok for Government

The Pentagon has launched ChatGPT Mil and Grok for Government, custom versions of the popular generative AI tools, for its 3 million civilian and military personnel via the GenAI.mil portal. These secure, unclassified tools are tailored for "warfighter needs" and exempt from data collection, supporting administrative tasks, logistics, and planning. The move highlights the Pentagon's efforts to leverage commercial AI while avoiding data security risks, and its focus on other providers following a dispute with Anthropic over safety guardrails.

Policy & business Judge Rebukes HHS Over AI Use in Cutting Prevention Grants

A judge has reprimanded the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) for its use of AI in deciding to cut teen pregnancy prevention grants. This ruling highlights concerns about transparency and fairness in governmental AI applications. It suggests that AI deployment in public services needs careful oversight to avoid biased or unjustified outcomes.
