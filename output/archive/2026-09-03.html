Model releases Google’s WeatherNext 3 AI Model Improves Forecasts

Google DeepMind and Google Research released WeatherNext 3, a new AI weather model that significantly improves forecasting accuracy and resolution. It achieves a 60% improvement in rain evaluations over WeatherNext 2 and can predict hourly forecasts down to 5 km resolution. WeatherNext 3, which is 2.4 times larger than its predecessor, will integrate into Google products like Search, Maps, and Gemini.

Policy & business Palo Alto Networks acquires Console for $500M

Palo Alto Networks acquired Console, an AI agent startup automating IT help desk tasks, for $500 million in cash and stock. Console had raised $29 million since its 2024 founding and was valued at $157 million before the acquisition. Palo Alto Networks plans to integrate Console's agentic functionality into its Cortex platform for autonomous security outcomes.

Policy & business UK Lords call for AI 'kill switch' powers

A group of UK peers is advocating for the government to have 'kill switch' powers to deactivate powerful AI systems and shut down data centers in national security threats. Lord Tim Clement-Jones proposed this amendment to the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill, aiming to create a 'vital safety net' against runaway AI. Separately, an AI Security Bill is also being introduced, which could halt superintelligent AI development in the UK, making it the first G7 country to do so.

Tools & products BBC travels in self-driving Uber around London

Uber has launched its self-driving minicab service in London, with customers now able to book rides in Wayve's autonomous vehicles.
