Mitra-v2: Tabular Foundation Model Excels on Synthetic Data

Mitra-v2 is a new tabular foundation model trained solely on a large, diverse synthetic dataset, achieving state-of-the-art performance on over 300 real-world classification and regression problems. Built on a small 2D Transformer, it supports longer contexts and larger feature spaces, outperforming Mitra-v1 and setting new benchmarks on TabArena and TALENT.

Research REFINE: Personalized Medical Concept Representation

REFINE is a new framework that uses Large Language Models to create personalized medical concept representations from Text-attributed Knowledge Graphs (TKGs). This approach addresses challenges in deciding how much KG context to use and aligning semantic information with patient-specific relational structures, which is crucial for Electronic Health Record (EHR) prediction.

Research Training-Free Expert Halving in MoE Models

A new method allows for training-free halving of activated experts in fine-grained Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models by normalizing by the probability mass of a larger top-k set (k2) than the activated experts (k1). On Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, this reduces compute by half while preserving MMLU score (0.35 points drop vs 4.65 points for standard renormalization).

Research Random Attention: Efficient KV Cache Eviction for LLMs

Random Attention is a new KV cache eviction method that keeps the prompt and evicts uniformly at random within each attention head, without computing importance scores. Across four models and six reasoning tasks, it matches the strongest prior evictor while achieving 32-43% higher throughput in vLLM deployment, by recognizing the prompt as the most crucial part of the cache.

Research Puffin-World: Unified Multimodal Model with Native 3D States

Puffin-World is a unified multimodal architecture that integrates physical understanding, spatial simulation, and 3D world generation and reconstruction. It models physics, geometry, and appearance using a unified Omni-Camera representation, and propagates physical dynamics across future frames to enable physically consistent and visually stable world generation.

Research SolarWM: Open Data & Scalable Training for Video World Models

SolarWM is an open foundation for building interactive video world models, tackling challenges in training across heterogeneous data sources and video backbones. It features a reconfigurable multi-source data engine that unifies 1.43 million clips from 10 datasets, along with a backbone-native adaptation framework for consistent supervision and improved reproducibility.
