Mistral raises €3B as sovereign AI becomes big business

French AI lab Mistral AI has raised €3 billion (approximately $3.58 billion) in a Series D round, valuing the company at over €21 billion ($24.39 billion). Led by Samsung Electronics, this funding is the largest equity fundraising ever for a European tech company and will be used to scale compute capacity, infrastructure, and accelerate commercial growth amid a focus on sovereign AI.

Policy & business New UK Law to Force Tech Firms to Stop Child Nude Images

The UK government plans to introduce new legislation compelling tech companies like Apple and Google to prevent children from taking or sharing nude images on their devices. This move comes after existing proposals from tech firms were deemed insufficient to address the scale of the crisis. The law aims to enforce device-level blocks and similar restrictions within apps used by children.
