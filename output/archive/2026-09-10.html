Policy & business Maven Robotics Raises $100M to Steal Robot Deployment

The company specializes in 'mixed palletizing,' where robots pick and arrange diverse goods onto pallets for retail delivery, boasting 99%+ uptime and beating out rivals for a major consumer goods deal. Policy & business California Governor Newsom Signs First-in-Nation AI Safeguards

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed two bills, SB 813 and AB 1405, establishing first-in-the-nation AI safeguards. These laws create a framework for independent, third-party audits and assessments of AI systems to ensure transparency and accountability. The Governor also urged the federal government to implement robust national AI regulations.
