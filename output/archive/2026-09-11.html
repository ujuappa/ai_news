IBM and NASA Release Open-Source Lunar Foundation Model

IBM and NASA have openly released the NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model, a pioneering AI model for lunar scientific exploration. Trained on extensive lunar observation data, it aims to help scientists analyze complex, multi-instrument data to support a sustained human presence on the Moon. The model has shown a 22% reduction in error for identifying potential lunar ice deposits compared to the SwinV2-B (ImageNet) model and a 3% improvement in capturing volcanic features.

Policy & business AI Workforce Commission Names 20 Commissioners

The AEI–Urban Commission on AI and the Future of the American Workforce has appointed 20 commissioners to advise policymakers on AI's impact on jobs. Co-chaired by Gina Raimondo and Paul Ryan, the commission will develop evidence-based policy playbooks to help workers, employers, and governments adapt to the economic changes brought by AI. The commissioners bring diverse expertise from business, labor, government, and academia to assess AI's reshaping of jobs, skills, benefits, and earnings.

Policy & business UK lawmakers urge Burnham to block creation of ASI
