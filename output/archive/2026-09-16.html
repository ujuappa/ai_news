TypeSafe AI Releases Jev: New System One Model

TypeSafe AI has launched Jev, a new "System One Model" designed for fast, structured decision-making, differentiating itself from traditional LLMs. Jev prioritizes structured outputs over string generation, aiming to be two orders of magnitude faster and more efficient while eliminating hallucinations in its specific task domain. The model uses a new architecture and training method called Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions (RLCD).

Policy & business OpenAI, Anthropic, Google have been in talks on AI safety

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind have been engaged in talks for weeks regarding AI safety, as confirmed by OpenAI's global policy chief Chris Lehane. This collaboration, which aims to address catastrophic AI risks and potentially establish a standards body, has raised antitrust concerns, though Lehane stated a government waiver isn't needed.
