Crusoe raises $3.9B to build massive data centers

Crusoe, a data center developer, raised $3.9 billion in Series F funding at a $30.9 billion valuation, co-led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital, and Valor Equity Partners. The company plans to use the capital to finance existing data center projects, including a large site for OpenAI in Abilene, Texas, and to develop modular 'AI factories' called Spark for rapid deployment.

Chinese AI startup Manus is reportedly seeking $500 million in funding at a $4 billion valuation, resuming independent operations after its $2 billion merger with Meta was blocked by Beijing due to regulatory concerns. Manus, known for its AI agents and tools, had to buy back its shares and delete user data generated during the Meta acquisition period to comply with regulations.

Policy & business Microsoft exec called AI scraping ‘largest theft of labor’

Unredacted court filings in The New York Times' copyright lawsuit against OpenAI and Microsoft reveal a Microsoft executive privately called AI scraping "the largest theft of labor in human history." The documents suggest that AI training practices, including bypassing paywalls and stripping copyright notices, led to a 93% drop in click-through rates for The New York Times via Microsoft's Copilot, challenging OpenAI's fair use defense by demonstrating market harm.

Policy & business Researchers used Anthropic’s Claude to hack into OpenAI

Security researchers used Anthropic's Claude to hack into OpenAI, exploiting two critical vulnerabilities within OpenAI's third-party forum software, Discourse, and an outdated image processing library, libheif. The Hacktron AI team received a $6,500 bug-bounty award after gaining access to OpenAI employee ChatGPT accounts, highlighting the security risks even advanced AI companies face from readily available tools.

Policy & business Google DeepMind launches institute to widen the AGI debate

Google DeepMind has launched the DeepMind Institute to foster broader discussions on artificial general intelligence (AGI), bringing together diverse perspectives from within Google and the global research community. The institute's inaugural essays address topics such as economic policies for AGI, preserving human-readable model reasoning, principles for human flourishing, and a framework for evaluating frontier AI models, with Demis Hassabis proposing a US-led standards body for AI model assessment.

Model releases PrismML hopes its tiny LLM will change how we all use AI

PrismML, an AI lab founded by Caltech researchers, released Bonsai 2 27B, a compressed version of Alibaba's Qwen3.8 27B model, reducing its size to 5.9 GB while retaining 98% of its aggregate benchmark scores. This model is small enough to run on PCs and high-end smartphones, demonstrating PrismML's unique ternary weight compression technology that aims to make capable, high-performing LLMs accessible for local deployment.
