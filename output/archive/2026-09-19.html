Policy & business AI Hallucination Nearly Caused US Military Operation

A US military operation against a Chinese vessel was nearly launched due to intelligence hallucinated by an AI chatbot. The AI tool misidentified the ship's cargo, leading to military aircraft being deployed before the error was discovered, highlighting the dangers of AI errors in critical decision-making without sufficient human oversight.

Model releases ChatGPT Inventor's TypeSafe AI Releases Jev Model

Diogo Almeida, an OpenAI researcher who co-invented RLHF, launched TypeSafe AI and released Jev, a new transformer-based model. Jev, unlike LLMs, produces 'calibrated decisions' (probabilities), making it fast, cheap, and hallucination-free, proving five to 18 times faster than OpenAI's Luna 5.6 for classification and significantly cheaper than Gemini.

Policy & business Vals Raises $40M from Andreessen Horowitz for AI Benchmarking

Vals, an AI startup founded in 2024 by Rayan Krishnan, has raised $40 million in a Series A round led by Andreessen Horowitz, following a seed round led by 8VC and Bloomberg Beta. The company aims to set a new standard for AI benchmarking by not publicly disclosing test materials and evaluating models on complex, domain-specific tasks in fields like law and finance, rather than abstract intelligence.

Policy & business Vantora Raises $100M for Physical AI Startups

Vantora (formerly UP.Labs), a startup studio, has secured a $100 million investment from Silversmith Capital Partners to focus on building 'physical AI' startups exclusively for corporate customers. This new strategy allows corporate partners to integrate these startups into their core businesses, unlocking sensitive use cases in industrial manufacturing and oil and gas that couldn't be commercialized broadly.

Policy & business Accenture to Be Anthropic's First Embedded Evaluator

Anthropic announced that Accenture's AI division, Faculty, will serve as its first embedded evaluator, scrutinizing its models and staff. Both companies plan to invest at least $1 billion over five years in this project, aimed at red-teaming models, conducting alignment assessments, and testing safeguards, a surprising choice given Accenture's lack of bleeding-edge AI research background.
