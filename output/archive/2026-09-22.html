Policy & business British Columbia sues OpenAI over school shooting

British Columbia is suing OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman in California, alleging that a mass school shooting could have been prevented if the company had warned police about the shooter's use of ChatGPT for planning. The lawsuit seeks damages for recovery efforts and changes to how OpenAI handles potentially violent conversations.

Policy & business OpenAI forms math advisory group, claims 100+ problems solved

OpenAI has formed a new independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence, hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study, to increase mathematician input into its research. This follows OpenAI's claim of its model resolving over 100 open math problems, including the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem, a claim that has sparked debate among mathematicians. The group will advise but not direct research pace.

Tools & products AstroForge puts AI in command of its next spacecraft

AstroForge is developing an autonomous control stack called "Solo," a transformer-based AI model, to command its next spacecraft in 2027. This innovative approach aims to overcome the limitations of ground control for deep space missions, especially for startups with fewer resources. The company has raised $56 million in venture funding.

Former UK deputy prime minister Nick Clegg, a board member of Nscale, could gain approximately $40 million (£30 million) from the company's planned US stock market listing. He owns over 917,000 shares in the datacentre startup, which is reportedly seeking a $35 billion valuation.

Policy & business Opposition to data center construction disrupting $68B projects

A Data & Society report reveals growing public opposition to data center construction, disrupting $68 billion worth of projects in Q2 2026. This sentiment is fueled by a distrust of "inevitable" AI narratives and concerns rooted in past industrial traumas in places like Pennsylvania. Trade unions, however, remain a key source of support due to job potential.
