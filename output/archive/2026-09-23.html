China Builds Huge Data Centers for AI Ambitions

China is rapidly building massive data centers in Inner Mongolia, demonstrating "China speed" in infrastructure development, to power its national AI ambitions. These facilities are critical for China's goal of embedding AI across 90% of industries and society by 2030, a key aspect of its competition with the US. The country is also fostering a strong talent pool, with more engineers returning from the US due to tensions and immigration restrictions, enabling a robust domestic AI ecosystem.

Policy & business Snorkel AI Triples Valuation to $3.5B with $350M Series E

Snorkel AI has raised $350 million in Series E funding at a $3.5 billion valuation, nearly tripling its previous valuation of $1.3 billion. The company, which provides data-as-a-service including training datasets and simulated environments, now reports an annualized revenue run rate of $375 million. This growth is driven by the high demand for training data from AI labs and corporations, with Snorkel utilizing a hybrid approach of software-generated and expert-curated data.

Model releases OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Sol and Luna: Lower Cost, Fewer Mistakes

OpenAI has launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna, updated versions of its smaller models, focusing on efficiency and affordability. These new models are half the cost of their predecessors, GPT-5.6 Sol and Luna, due to improvements in caching and inference. OpenAI claims GPT-6 Sol makes about half as many factual mistakes as before and boasts Astra-level reliability, outperforming Anthropic's top models in internal evaluations.

Policy & business OpenAI Gives Ukraine AI Cyber Defense Tools

OpenAI is providing its AI cyber defense system, Daybreak, to the Ukrainian government for free to help protect civilian infrastructure from cyber-attacks. Ukraine, which recorded nearly 6,000 attacks in 2025, will gain access to OpenAI's advanced GPT 5.6 Sol model. This initiative is a response to the significant volume of Russian cyber operations against Ukraine, offering a valuable force multiplier for the nation's defense efforts.

Policy & business Ema Raises $77M as AI Disrupts Enterprise Software

Ema, a startup automating corporate processes with teams of AI agents, raised $77 million in a Series B round, tripling its valuation from 2024. The funding, led by Creaegis with increased stakes from Accel, Section 32, and Prosus, brings total funding to $140 million. Ema's 'AI employees' orchestrate multiple AI agents across existing applications, aiming to reduce reliance on traditional enterprise software and potentially replace SaaS products by leveraging over 150 models.
