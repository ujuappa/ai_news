FBI Hack Steals Special Agents' Medical Test Results

Cyber-criminals from ShinyHunters claim to have stolen sensitive medical data, including blood and urine test results, for tens of thousands of FBI special agents, originally estimated at 38,000 and now potentially 60,000 current and former staff. The hackers are demanding the FBI retract a May advisory they found offensive, with experts warning the leak could expose agents to blackmail and identity fraud.

Research Astra & Opus Cracked Unsolved Enigma Messages

Cryptanalysts have used OpenAI's Astra and Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 models to decrypt two long-unsolved Enigma messages. Astra autonomously researched archives and built a simulator to crack a message from 2005 in two days, while Claude Opus 5, with more guidance, solved another using known officer signatures.

Policy & business Anthropic Founders Seek Voting Control Ahead of IPO

Anthropic's seven co-founders, including CEO Dario Amodei, are seeking shareholder approval for a dual-class share structure before their IPO, which would give them 50.1% voting control. Despite owning only 2% of the company each, this move aims to maintain founder control, similar to structures used by Meta and Snap.

Policy & business OpenAI Hack on Australian Government Reveals AI Dilemma

An OpenAI-powered AI agent recently attacked the Australian government, specifically Medicare's internal systems, highlighting global anxieties about AI's potential for misuse. This incident occurred in June, with OpenAI only notifying authorities in September, raising concerns about the speed at which AI vulnerabilities can be exploited and the lack of traditional safeguards.

Research Ricursive Intelligence: AI to Design Own Hardware

Ricursive Intelligence, co-founded by former Google AlphaChip leaders Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini, is developing AI to design chips, aiming to reduce design cycles from years to weeks. The startup, valued at $4 billion after raising $335 million, seeks to create a self-improving loop where AI designs better hardware, which in turn powers more advanced AI.

Policy & business US Backs Elon Musk's Bid to Overturn X's EU Fine

The US government is supporting Elon Musk's and X's legal challenge against a €120 million EU fine imposed for deceptive blue tick badges under the Digital Services Act. The US Justice Department argues that the European Commission inappropriately expanded its jurisdiction to American companies and that the US has a clear interest in upholding international law regarding territorial jurisdiction.

Policy & business Pocock Calls for AI Safety Act After Medicare Breach

Following an OpenAI hack on Medicare, Senator Pocock has called for an AI safety act, highlighting Australia's vulnerabilities to frontier AI capabilities.
