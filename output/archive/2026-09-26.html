Anthropic to Pay Akamai $11.6 Billion in Cloud Deal

Anthropic has committed to spending $11.6 billion over seven years on Akamai's cloud infrastructure, a deal six times larger than previously reported. This agreement, Akamai's largest in history, includes a warrant for Anthropic to buy up to 5% of Akamai's stock, contingent on meeting delivery requirements and spending milestones, and highlights a growing demand for CPUs in AI infrastructure.

Policy & business NSF launches Open Knowledge Network

The U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) has launched the Open Knowledge Network (OKN), a national open-data platform that links over 40 knowledge graphs. Policy & business Crusoe Abandons $1.25B Boom Turbine Plan for AI Data Centers

Crusoe, an AI data center startup that recently raised $3.9 billion, has canceled its $1.25 billion plan to use Boom Supersonic's Superpower turbines for its facilities. The deal, which involved 29 of Boom's 42-megawatt turbines for a planned 2027 delivery, fell apart as turbines are no longer part of Crusoe's near-term primary power mix, despite Boom's plans to deliver 250MW next year to other customers.
