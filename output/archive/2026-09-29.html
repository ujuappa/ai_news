Policy & business AMD Will Acquire Fei-Fei Li’s World Labs for $8.2 Billion

AMD is set to acquire World Labs, a developer of deep learning models for understanding physical reality, in an $8.2 billion deal. This acquisition will bring World Labs founder Fei-Fei Li to AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist, aiming to accelerate the scaling of their technical breakthroughs and integrate model research with systems and compute. The move is expected to bolster AMD's competition with Nvidia in developing an AI-specific chip ecosystem, particularly for generative AI on robotic platforms, which heavily relies on synthetic data from world models.
