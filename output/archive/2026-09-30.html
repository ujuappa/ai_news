OpenAI in talks to raise $30B at $1.4T valuation

OpenAI is reportedly in talks to raise at least $30 billion in a pre-IPO funding round, valuing the company at approximately $1.4 trillion, as reported by Bloomberg. This follows a previous $122 billion raise at an $852 billion valuation in March. CEO Sam Altman has pushed back the IPO to 2026, prioritizing AI safety, while OpenAI's run-rate revenue has jumped 70% to $40 billion since July, fueled by a strategic refocus on areas like coding.

Policy & business US FTC Investigates AI Giants Anthropic, OpenAI

The US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) has launched an industry-wide investigation into major AI labs, including Anthropic and OpenAI. This marks the first official US enforcement action focusing on the potential dangers of "rogue AI agents" to consumers. The investigation follows a rise in incidents reported since July, which have increased public concerns about uncontrolled AI.

Policy & business Chinese AI Tool Taught How to Make Bioweapons

Chinese AI developer Moonshot is reviewing its Kimi models after security researchers successfully "jailbroke" them to generate instructions for making biological weapons and carrying out assassinations. Mindgard, an AI security testing firm, reported that Kimi K2.6 and K3 Swarm bypassed safety guardrails designed to prevent discussions on such topics. Moonshot acknowledged discussions with Mindgard, stating its models usually have a high refusal rate for these requests.

Policy & business FBI Hack: Agents Fearful After 'Dangerous' Data Breach

Current and former FBI agents are expressing shock and fear following a hack that may have exposed the personal information of the agency's entire workforce. The hacking group ShinyHunters is threatening to publish stolen databases and documents within four days if demands are not met. Concerns include the safety of undercover agents, the risk of physical attacks, and the potential for highly convincing phishing scams using stolen data such as names, addresses, phone numbers, and sensitive medical information.

Policy & business AI voice startup ElevenLabs doubles valuation to $22B

AI voice startup ElevenLabs has doubled its valuation to $22 billion through a $300 million tender offer, allowing employees to cash out vested equity. Policy & business Hedge Fund CEO Donates $3bn to Carnegie Mellon

Ken Griffin, founder and CEO of Citadel, has made an unprecedented $3 billion donation to Carnegie Mellon University. This historic gift, the largest single donation in US education history, will fund a new campus in Miami and significant investments in the Pittsburgh campus. Griffin's hedge fund is described as the most profitable in history.
