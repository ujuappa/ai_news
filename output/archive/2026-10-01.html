Model releases Google Releases Gemini 4 Argon: Most Powerful Model Yet

Google has launched Gemini 4 Argon, touted as its most powerful AI model to date, specifically designed for cybersecurity, coding, and general reasoning. Currently available only to select cyber partners through Google's Fairwind Program, Argon excels at autonomously finding, validating, and patching software vulnerabilities. Google claims it outperforms OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra and Anthropic's Fable and Opus on various AI benchmarks.

Flow Engineering, an AI startup offering tools for hardware design, has secured a $50 million Series B round at a $750 million valuation. Policy & business Newsom Signs Laws Protecting CA Workers from AI Threat

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed new laws to protect workers from AI threats, including potential job losses and workplace surveillance. The legislation bans employers from using AI to predict emotional states with biometric data, mandates written notices for AI-driven mass layoffs, and prohibits AI from making firing decisions. Newsom criticized Donald Trump for failing to pass comprehensive federal AI regulations.
