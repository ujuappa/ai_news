California No Robo Bosses Act bans AI-only worker firings

California has enacted the "No Robo Bosses Act of 2026," becoming the first state to restrict AI's role in employee termination and disciplinary decisions. The law, SB 947, mandates human corroboration with additional information when AI is the primary basis for such decisions and requires employers to inform affected workers about AI involvement and provide a human contact. This aims to prevent AI errors and bias, ensuring human oversight in critical employment judgments.

Research GPT-6 Astra Robot Agents: 14% Higher Success, 65% Fewer Tokens

PyRUA-Lean is an interactive code-execution framework that improves VLM robot agents by coupling feedback-driven primitive composition with selective observation. It achieves a 14% higher success rate with 65% fewer tokens across 700 simulated tasks by allowing agents to compose classical robot primitives and learned VLA policies, returning only explicitly requested feedback for replanning.

Research Tiny LoRA Fixes Early Stoppage in Transformers

Transformers often stop processing context references too early, typically after 1.4-3.6 lines. A small rank-8 LoRA applied to one early layer, with all other model weights frozen, significantly extends this computation, with Qwen3-8B improving from 15.5% to 99% accuracy on 24-line chains.

Tools & products CoreWeave Ships Forge — GPU Compute Landlord Becomes Agent

CoreWeave launched Forge, a unified development layer that integrates the AI improvement lifecycle—Run, Observe, Curate, Improve, Evaluate—directly on its GPU infrastructure. Forge consolidates existing tools like Weights & Biases and OpenPipe with new services, including Model Distillation and ARIA, a research agent. Key components like Agent Lens, Sandboxes, and Registry position CoreWeave beyond a compute supplier, offering agent execution, observability, and asset management close to the GPUs, which could significantly impact performance and switching costs.
