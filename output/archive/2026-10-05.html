Reflection Debuts Beam: Open-Weight AI Model Rivals Chinese Rivals

Reflection AI launched Beam, a 501-billion-parameter open-weight AI model with 23 billion active parameters, claiming it matches leading Chinese open models on advanced reasoning benchmarks at significantly lower compute costs. Policy & business OpenAI to Watermark ChatGPT Text in the EU

OpenAI will implement an invisible watermark, called textGrain, on ChatGPT and Codex generated text in the EU to comply with the EU AI Act's transparency rules. This watermark subtly shapes word choices, allowing detection by an algorithm without impacting model performance, though it can be removed by significant editing.

Policy & business OpenAI: EU Text Provenance Rules

OpenAI has outlined its strategy for implementing text provenance rules under EU regulations, focusing on watermarking text generated by its models. This approach details where watermarks are applied, how detection mechanisms function, and why initial access is being granted to researchers to foster understanding and adaptation. The initiative aims to enhance transparency and accountability for AI-generated content in compliance with European Union directives.
