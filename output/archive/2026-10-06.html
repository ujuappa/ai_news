Mistral's New 1T Model Aims to Leapfrog Rivals

Mistral AI has released Mistral Large 4 (ML4), a new 1 trillion-parameter multimodal model, aiming to compete with both open and closed AI rivals. Nicknamed Le Chonk, ML4 is currently accessible via a public endpoint but its weights are planned for open release in three weeks after safety testing. Mistral trained ML4 on 4,000 Nvidia GPUs, significantly less than competitors, and hopes it will excel in cybersecurity, finance, and chip design, following a recent Series D funding at a €21 billion valuation.

Model releases Kandinsky 6.0 Video: Synchronized Video and Audio

Kandinsky 6.0 Video is a new family of foundation diffusion models for synchronized text-to-audio-video generation, available in Lite (3B parameters) and Pro (29B parameters) versions. These models generate 5-second video clips with synchronized 44 kHz audio, including lip-sync, in Full-HD (1920x1080) resolution, building on Kandinsky 5.0's video generation capabilities.

Policy & business Deutsche Telekom Targets €2.5 Billion in AI Savings by 2030

Deutsche Telekom projects €2.5 billion in indirect cost savings by 2030, driven by the expansion of AI and automation across its operations. The company anticipates €1.1 billion in gross savings outside the US by 2027, with some of these funds reinvested into Germany's digital infrastructure. Deutsche Telekom also aims to increase AI-related revenue from businesses outside the US to approximately €800 million by 2030.
