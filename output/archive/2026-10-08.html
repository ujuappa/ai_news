White House Blocks Microsoft from Foreign Worker Hiring Program

The White House has blocked Microsoft and several other major tech firms, including Adobe, Cognizant, and Infosys, from a key foreign worker hiring program. Vice President JD Vance accused these companies of misusing the H1B visa program and the Permanent Labor Certification Program (PERM), alleging they replace American workers with "foreign indentured servants." The US government will not process new or pending PERM applications from these companies, although Microsoft stated that the vast majority of its US employees are Americans and 80% of its H1B filings are for existing staff.

Policy & business AI Leaderboard Arena Nears $3.1B Valuation

AI leaderboard Arena has nearly doubled its valuation to $3.1 billion in 10 months after raising a $200 million Series B round. The company, which originated as a UC Berkeley research project, reached $100 million in annualized run-rate revenue in June by crowdsourcing AI model rankings and offering commercial AI evaluation services. Arena also introduced a new alignment leaderboard to rank models on issues like unauthorized actions and deceptive completions.

Policy & business China's Manus Raises Over $500M After Meta Split

China's AI lab Manus has raised over $500 million in its first funding round since its acquisition by Meta was blocked by Chinese authorities. The parent company, Butterfly Effect, saw investments from Boyu Capital and IDG Capital, among others. Manus, which offers AI products and agents including the recently launched Manus 2.0 and Cue, is reportedly considering a public listing in Hong Kong.

Policy & business Nous Research Confirms $1.5B Valuation, Launches Enterprise AI

Nous Research confirmed raising a $90 million Series B round at a $1.5 billion valuation, bringing total funding to $158 million. The startup, known for its open-source Hermes Agent, will use the capital to expand into the enterprise sector with "Hermes for Businesses," offering customized AI agents for multi-step workflows. Nous Research expects to exceed $100 million in annualized revenue by the end of 2026.

Tools & products Google Brings Agentic AI to Gemini, Starting With Businesses

Google is introducing agentic AI capabilities to Gemini, initially for businesses, allowing it to perform tasks beyond just answering questions. The new unified agent, accessible via Google Cloud, can plan work, use custom skills, and connect to various business systems like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack. Users can track Gemini's progress in a "tasks inbox" and choose models, including third-party options like Anthropic's Claude.

Tools & products Microsoft Releases New Nvidia-Chip AI PCs, Revamped Windows 11

Microsoft has released new Nvidia-chip AI PCs, including the Surface Laptop Ultra (starting at $2,600) and the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box (starting at $6,000), running a revamped Windows 11 with "Execution Containers" for sandboxing AI agents. These devices are designed to run AI models locally on-device, leveraging Nvidia's RTX Spark chip. Microsoft is targeting developers, offering up to $1,000 off for MacBook Pro trade-ins, and Dell also announced its XPS 16 Creator Edition PC.

Policy & business OpenAI Revenue Reportedly $20 Billion Less Than Projected

OpenAI's annualized revenue is reportedly approaching $50 billion, which is $20 billion less than a previously projected $70 billion figure. This adjustment comes amidst efforts to compare its financials with companies like Anthropic, which use different revenue calculation methods. The revised figures could impact the company's IPO plans, now reportedly pushed to early 2027.

Policy & business Cal AI Founder Raises $10M for New AI Agent Startup Persona

Zach Yadegari, the 19-year-old founder of Cal AI, has launched a new AI agent startup called Persona, raising $10 million in funding. Persona will offer a personal AI assistant with an upcoming $179 wearable band that activates with a button or wrist flick, distinguishing it from always-listening ambient AI. The startup emphasizes user privacy, with data encrypted in Persona's cloud and a business model that incorporates unbiased advertising during shopping research.
