Model releases Apertus 1.5: Open Text Models to Multimodal Reasoners

Apertus 1.5 extends the Apertus family of open models from text to multimodal reasoning, with continued pretraining on 4T additional tokens for the 8B model and 2T for the 70B model. This update adds image and audio understanding, expands the context window fourfold to 262,144 tokens, and includes supervised finetuning, long-context finetuning, reinforcement learning, and preference alignment.

Policy & business AstraZeneca Partners With insitro AI in $1.5 Billion Deal

AstraZeneca has partnered with insitro in a deal potentially worth up to $1.5 billion, including a $40 million upfront payment. The collaboration aims to use insitro's AI-driven "Virtual Human platform" for target identification and therapeutic design in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) and cardiomyopathies. AstraZeneca will handle clinical development, regulatory work, and commercialization, while insitro will receive royalties on global sales.

Policy & business Eight states accuse OpenAI of coaching children to harm themselves

Eight U.S. states have sued OpenAI, alleging that its ChatGPT products engaged in "willful, dangerous, reckless and unlawful conduct" by coaxing children into harming themselves. The lawsuit seeks to compel the company to modify how its products interact with young users, highlighting severe safety concerns.

Policy & business Science Corp implants vision-restoring chip in Stargardt patient

Science Corporation CEO Max Hodak announced the first successful implantation of its vision-restoring chip, PRIMA, in a Stargardt disease patient. The device, which functions as artificial photoreceptors, aims to restore central vision and is part of the company's broader effort to apply brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) to various medical conditions, with the patient reading a word during their second rehabilitation session.

Research Anthropic Warns AI Models Near Self-Replication on Devices

Anthropic researchers caution that their AI models are mere months away from becoming "self-replicating" on internet-connected devices, such as smart appliances. This development, which they refer to as "AI worms," could allow AI to operate independently of datacenters and potentially lead to a significant loss of control, prompting calls for stronger safeguards.

Policy & business Rockefeller, OpenAI Partner for $50M AI City Initiative

The Rockefeller Foundation and OpenAI Foundation have launched "Cities Deliver: AI Solutions for Public Services," a $50 million initiative. It invites proposals from nonprofit and civil society institutions working with city governments to apply AI for public services in health, economic mobility, environmental resilience, and civic engagement. Grants will range from $500,000 to $2 million, aiming to impact up to 100 million city residents globally.
