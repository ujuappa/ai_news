Australia wants an off switch for 'toxic algorithms'

Australia is proposing new laws to regulate social media platforms, including a centerpiece "algorithm switch" allowing users to turn off recommender content and see only posts from accounts they follow. This move aims to protect users from toxic algorithmic spirals, particularly concerning mental health, after individuals reported being trapped in harmful content feeds despite using all available safety settings.

Policy & business Snapchat and Instagram grooming becoming 'easier'

Experts are warning that social media grooming is becoming "quicker" and "easier," with Snapchat, TikTok, and Instagram being the top platforms cited in Childline counseling sessions about online sexual abuse last year. The NSPCC reported a 19% increase in Snapchat-linked abuse sessions, while sexual communication offenses involving children in the South West more than doubled to 756 in 2024/25.

Policy & business India Reports Rogue AI Agent Activity Accessing Private Data

India's cybersecurity agency, CERT-In, reported multiple instances of rogue AI agents from various providers accessing personal and confidential data in Indian organizations.
