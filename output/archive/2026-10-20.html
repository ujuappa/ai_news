Eka Robotics Valued at $1.45B After $150M Series A for Robots

Eka Robotics, founded by MIT professor Pulkit Agrawal and former Google DeepMind researcher Tuomas Haarnoja, secured a $150 million Series A round, valuing the company at $1.45 billion. The startup's manipulation architecture, featuring a force-sensitive gripper and a vision-force-action AI model, demonstrates "superhuman performance" in dexterous manipulation, capable of picking up 1,500 objects per hour with over 95% reliability. This innovation addresses the long-standing challenge of robotic object handling.

Policy & business Eric Schmidt Reportedly Owns 1% of Anthropic, Valued at $2T

Former Google CEO Eric Schmidt reportedly owns about 1% of Anthropic, a significant stake following his Series A investment in 2021 when the company was valued at $461 million. This ownership is half of what co-founder and CEO Dario Amodei holds. If Anthropic's valuation reaches up to $2 trillion as expected for its IPO, Schmidt's stake could be worth $20 billion, considerably boosting his $57.5 billion net worth.

Policy & business Meta’s Muse Hits 10M U.S. Downloads in 40 Days

Meta’s AI companion, Muse, has achieved 10 million U.S. downloads in its first 40 days, making it one of only 12 apps to reach this milestone. This success is driven by Meta's cross-promotion across its platforms and significant advertising investment, with 39% of its October ad spend allocated to Muse. Muse also boasts higher 7-day (30%) and 14-day (23%) retention rates compared to rivals like ChatGPT (19%, 16%).

Policy & business Otter.ai Raises $320M from General Catalyst for Meeting Tool

Otter.ai, a meeting note-taking tool, raised $320 million from General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund, valuing its growth and aiming for enterprise expansion and product innovation. The company, which achieved $100 million in annual recurring revenue last year, has served 40 million users to date. The funding will support its efforts to transform meeting transcripts into actionable insights and streamline workflows.

Tools & products Microsoft warns AI agents are like insider threats

Microsoft has introduced Guardian Agents, a new security feature under Project Perception, to protect enterprises from unpredictable AI agents. While originally focused on external threats, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella now warns that internally deployed AI agents pose "insider threats" due to their unpredictability, even from trusted model providers. The company argues enterprises need separate, independent systems to monitor and control AI agents given access to sensitive data and critical actions.

Tools & products llm-d 1.0 Advances Open, Cost-Efficient AI Inference

The llm-d 1.0 open-source project has been released, aiming to make large language model inference more cost-efficient and scalable for production environments. Founded by Red Hat, IBM, Google Cloud, CoreWeave, and NVIDIA in 2025, and now governed by the CNCF, llm-d 1.0 introduces features like multi-tenant quality of service and zero-downtime operations. These advancements are designed to address the challenges and expenses associated with deploying AI at scale, especially for agentic workloads.
