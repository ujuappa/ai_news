Policy & business PHP Flaws Spark Global Cyber-Security Scramble

Critical vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) have been discovered in PHP, a programming language powering 70% of the world's websites, including Wikipedia and WordPress. These flaws, undetected for over a decade and rated 9.2 out of 10 in severity, could allow hackers to take control of affected web servers and steal sensitive data. The issues were brought to light with the help of AI, prompting a global cybersecurity scramble, with governments and organizations urging immediate patching or taking websites offline.

Model releases Canva Releases First Image Generation Model: Canva Craft

Canva has released its first image generation model, Canva Craft, developed by its research team to compete with models from Google, OpenAI, and Meta. Canva Craft generates high-resolution images up to 2K with legible text and precise editing capabilities, trained on human-designed templates from Canva's library rather than photographs.

Policy & business Zuckerberg Wins Meta Deal to Offset Possible CA Tax

Meta has struck a "Founder Alignment Agreement" with CEO Mark Zuckerberg to incentivize him to remain in California amid a proposed 5% state tax on billionaires. The deal provides Zuckerberg with more Meta shares, potentially worth around $10 billion, to offset the estimated tax burden on his over $200 billion fortune. Meta emphasized the importance of keeping Zuckerberg in California for leadership during the AI transformation, and the agreement remains active for eight years if he stays in the state.

Policy & business UK Addresses Global Cyber-Security Warning for PHP

The UK government is working to identify and mitigate exposure in its online services following the discovery of critical cybersecurity flaws in PHP, which powers approximately 30 million websites. These vulnerabilities, discovered with the aid of AI by researcher Ryan Austin, are considered the most serious he has found in 20 years, allowing attackers to potentially run arbitrary code and steal data. Governments globally are urging immediate patching or taking systems offline to prevent widespread exploitation.

Policy & business Blackstone's Khaira Says AI-Buildout Debt Fears Overblown

Blackstone's Jas Khaira asserts that fears regarding AI-buildout debt are overblown, as the firm positions itself centrally in the AI boom with its $1.3 trillion in assets under management. Blackstone provides significant loans to data centers and invests in frontier AI models, having pioneered a compute financing structure that lends to neoclouds like CoreWeave against customer contracts and GPU collateral.
