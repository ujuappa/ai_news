Google DeepMind Releases Genie 4 World Model

Google DeepMind has released Genie 4, its newest world model, now in research preview. This updated model generates more photorealistic and interactive worlds, includes real-time audio generation (music, ambient noises, dialogue), and allows for free-form edits during simulation. Genie 4 can maintain consistent scenes for up to two minutes and shows improved physics and humanlike characters, with tighter integration with Gemini for task planning and frame checking.

Research Frontier AI Labs Refute Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH)

OpenAI and Anthropic have both released solutions that they claim refute the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), a long-standing assumption in computer science about computational difficulty. OpenAI shared its proofs in a decentralized manner across different scientific communities, leading to varied reactions. Anthropic also released its own refutation, sparking discussion within the research community about the norms of AI contributions to fundamental scientific problems.

Policy & business GMO Internet Investigates Data Leak, ~$50M Ransom

Japanese internet giant GMO Internet is investigating a data leak from its Onamae cloud desktop service, which may have affected 27,000 servers. Suspected attackers have allegedly published customer data and demanded 8 billion yen (~$50 million). This incident follows GMO's report of a Chinese AI-enabled penetration testing tool, Artex, being used in hacks targeting Japanese and South Korean financial institutions, though a direct connection to the Onamae breach is not yet established.

Tools & products Opera Introduces WebAI Standard for Local Browser AI

Opera has introduced WebAI, a new web standard enabling users to run AI models directly in their browser without cloud reliance. This feature, available as an early preview in Opera One, supports open-weight models like Google's Gemma and Apple's on-device foundation model on Macs. WebAI offers enhanced privacy by keeping data on the user's device and allows developers to add AI features without costly infrastructure, though performance depends on device hardware.

Policy & business General Compute Raises $100M Series A, Valued at $800M

General Compute has raised $100 million in Series A funding, bringing its valuation to $800 million post-money, amidst a challenging climate for neocloud companies. The startup focuses on specialized inference chips from Nvidia rivals like SambaNova, Positron, and Cerebras, and repurposes former bitcoin-mining sites for AI workloads. With a $400 million deal for new chips and a $100 million ARR customer, the company aims to deploy 75 megawatts of computing capacity by 2027.
