Policy & business Binance Hit by $6.3 Billion Hack, North Korea Suspected

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, reported that $6.3 billion in digital assets were impacted by what is considered the largest crypto hack on record. While about half of the stolen assets have been frozen, many remain with the hackers, and Binance has pledged to cover all losses. Cybersecurity firms, including Check Point and Chainalysis, attribute the incident to North Korea's TraderTraitor (Lazarus Group), known for targeting cryptocurrency companies to fund weapons programs.

Policy & business AWS will be a $1T business, Jassy says — but first, it will spend big

Amazon CEO Andy Jassy reiterated his belief that AWS can become a $1 trillion revenue business, despite expecting to spend "meaningfully more" than $220 billion in cash capital expenditures in 2027. Jassy projected AWS growth north of 40% in both 2027 and 2028, driven by a "once-in-a-lifetime opportunity" in AI adoption. Investors, however, showed concern, with shares down about 6% after the earnings call, indicating skepticism about the high investment costs.

Policy & business OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings acquires ADT for $7.6B

Thrive Holdings, a holding company backed by OpenAI, has acquired physical security firm ADT for $10 per share, totaling about $7.6 billion in equity ($15.3 billion enterprise value). Thrive Holdings' mission is to revitalize acquired companies with AI, with OpenAI providing models, tech, and engineers. This acquisition aims to integrate advanced AI into ADT's smart home security business, building on ADT's existing partnerships with Apple and Google.
