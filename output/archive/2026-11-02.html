Policy & business Anthropic Warns It's Close to RSI Capability

Policy & business NYT Sues Brave Browser Over Content Scraping

The New York Times, along with other outlets, is suing the Brave browser for alleged content scraping and unlawful distribution of copyrighted material to AI companies like Anthropic, Perplexity, Mistral, xAI, and Cohere. The lawsuit claims Brave built a 30 billion web page index, bypassed paywalls, and blocked publisher ads while selling its own.
