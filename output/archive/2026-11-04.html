Model releases Axion 1.0: Open Agent Foundation Models, Simplified

Axion 1.0 is introduced as a fully open agent foundation model with strong and broad agentic capabilities, achieved through a simple three-stage training recipe. This process involves two lightweight supervised fine-tuning stages followed by reinforcement learning, without requiring mid-training, DPO, or on-policy distillation. The SFT stages develop self-contained reasoning and tool-dependent reasoning through model-aware data construction and scalable task pipelines.
