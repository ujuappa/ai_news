Policy & business Midterms signal bigger clash over data centers incoming

Following the US midterm elections where Democrats gained full control of Congress, public anger over data center construction has become a significant political factor. Voters in several states pushed back against AI buildout, with some localities passing bans, suggesting a potential for new limits on where data centers can be built and how much local communities influence these decisions. Analysts predict construction delays in certain states and increased odds for bills requiring data centers to cover their own power grid upgrades.

Policy & business Meta acquires AI coding startup Conductor

Meta has acquired Conductor, an AI coding startup that enables users to create software projects by deploying multiple agents in parallel workspaces. Conductor's founder and CEO, Charlie Holtz, announced the acquisition and hinted at "big, crazy plans for the next interface" with Meta, expecting something new by early next year. While Conductor's cloud service will shut down on November 20, its core features will remain operational until early next year.
