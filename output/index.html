Elorian's Glimpse Model Reasons With Images

Elorian, a Palo Alto AI startup, has released its first model, Glimpse, as a research preview, focusing on reasoning *with* images rather than just *about* them. Glimpse maintains active visual context during reasoning, allowing it to zoom or extract video frames, distinguishing it from models that convert images to tokens once. Elorian claims Glimpse, trained on hundreds of GPUs, outperforms GPT-6 Astra on scientific chart reasoning and video captioning, aiming to move from visual reasoning to robot control.

Model releases Meta Releases Muse Spark Open, New AI Model

Model releases Meta Launches Muse Spark Open, Mac-Compatible AI

Meta launched Muse Spark Open, an 816 billion parameter, 1 million token context window open-weight AI model designed to run on Macs, requiring at least 1 terabyte of RAM. Meta claims it's the 'most capable' open-weight model in the US, offering superior performance in complex coding tasks and agentic workflows like bug fixes and video editing. The model provides cost-efficiency, transparency, and flexibility compared to proprietary alternatives.

Model releases Jev Becomes Vercel's Fastest-Adopted AI Model

Jev, a decision model by TypeSafe AI, has become Vercel's fastest-adopted AI model ever, used by 35% of paid teams within two weeks of its September 15 launch. Designed for quickly classifying data with predetermined outputs, Jev is cheaper and faster than alternatives like Sonnet, prompting competing offerings from OpenAI and Amazon. Vercel's data also shows open-source models now account for over 70% of tokens processed and nearly 25% of spend, while frontier lab spending has grown tenfold, suggesting complementarity rather than competition.

Policy & business Russian Man Arrested for Deadly London Hospitals Hack

German police have arrested a 28-year-old Russian man, Vladimir K., accused of being a core member of the Qilin cyber-crime gang, which caused a deadly hack at London hospitals in 2024. The hack of blood testing company Synnovis disrupted services and was linked to a patient's death. German and British authorities are now investigating whether this incident could lead to an unlawful killing case, a potential world first in cyber-crime.

Research Claude Improves Quantum Circuit Design by Order of Magnitude

Anthropic's Claude AI has demonstrated significant improvements in designing efficient quantum circuits, outperforming published designs by an order of magnitude or more in some cases. By taking plain-language specifications and using an automated evaluator, Claude substantially reduces the quantum resources needed for useful applications, potentially accelerating the arrival of practical quantum computers.
