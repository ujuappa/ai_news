Policy & business PHP Armageddon: OpenAI, Google's Wiz Find Ancient Holes

The NSA warned of two critical, decades-old flaws in PHP, the programming language powering 70% of websites, found by AI models like OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and Google's Wiz Atlas and Gemini 4 Argon. Patches have been issued, with the NSA recommending taking unpatched sites offline due to the severity of the vulnerabilities. While AI helped discover these previously hard-to-exploit flaws, it also makes them easier for hackers to adapt, creating a race for websites to update to PHP 8.5.12 before exploits emerge.

Policy & business Chinese Hackers Spied on Thousands via ConnectWise Breach

Chinese hackers spied on thousands of companies after breaching IT giant ConnectWise, stealing credentials from its cloud backup service. Microsoft researchers attributed the rapid rate of around 3,000 sign-in attempts per minute to AI agents, specifically ARTEX, an open-source AI framework. ConnectWise has paused access to its affected service and is investigating the breach, which also saw ARTEX used in a compromise of South Korea's financial systems.
