Policy & business Apple CEO: Don't 'ship AI for AI’s sake'

Apple CEO John Ternus stated that the company will not "ship AI for AI's sake," emphasizing a focus on integrating AI where it genuinely improves user experience. He believes the best AI solutions are those users don't overtly recognize as AI, making life easier and more intuitive.

Policy & business Judge allows NAACP lawsuit over xAI pollution to move forward

A federal judge has allowed the NAACP lawsuit against xAI regarding alleged data center pollution to proceed, denying xAI's motion to dismiss.
