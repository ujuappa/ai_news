Black October: LLMJacking Exposes AI Infrastructure Credentials

Wiz Research reported a "Black October" in 2026, where nine AI developer platforms experienced security breaches involving credential theft. Attackers leveraged these stolen credentials, including those for Fireworks AI and Lightning AI, to run up over $100k in unauthorized usage, demonstrating a critical security gap in AI infrastructure. This coordinated campaign, termed "LLMJacking," highlights the escalating threat of agentic attacks and the lack of preparedness among enterprises to secure their AI initiatives.

Policy & business Nvidia May Cosign Billions for Nscale's AI Data Center

Nvidia is reportedly considering guaranteeing billions in debt financing for Nscale's AI data center in West Virginia, which has a $44.6 billion contract with Anthropic for its first building. This unprecedented move would see Nvidia co-signing customer loans to ensure demand for its next-generation Vera Rubin chips, indicating the extreme measures being taken to finance the AI infrastructure buildout amid investor jitters. The deal is crucial for Nscale, which aims for a $35 billion IPO despite project delays and a fraction of its planned chip deployment.

Policy & business Cracks Emerge in the AI Spending Boom

Investors are growing nervous about the AI spending boom, as major tech companies like Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft plan over $740 billion in capital expenditures this year. This anxiety has led to a Nasdaq fall of 2.9% and significant drops in chip stocks, with Alphabet's stock plummeting 12% to its worst day on record. The concern stems from rising negative free cash flow, mounting debt, and doubts about whether these massive investments will yield sufficient returns, especially as AI threatens the core businesses that fund these outlays.

Policy & business North Korea's Lazarus Group suspect in Binance hack

Two major blockchain analytics firms, TRM Labs and Chainalysis, have identified North Korea's Lazarus Group (TraderTraitor) with "high confidence" as the likely perpetrators of a $6.3 billion hack on Binance. This incident is part of a pattern where North Korea is accused of using cybercrime to finance itself, including the theft of $1.5 billion from Bybit last year. Binance initially suspected an engineer's laptop was compromised but later confirmed a wider breach, though 80% of stolen assets have been secured.

Policy & business Reddit shifts to logged-in experience amid AI search impact

Reddit is transitioning to a primarily "logged-in experience" to protect its data from AI scraping and enable more lucrative licensing deals with AI model providers. This strategic shift comes as search traffic referrals have decreased by over 1 million per week in October, impacting global daily active users, which were flat quarter-over-quarter at 130.3 million. Despite user declines, Reddit's revenue increased by 53% year-over-year to $895 million, driven by advertising and AI data-licensing.
