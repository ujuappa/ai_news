Gemini 4 Carbon: Google's next frontier model

Google has announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its latest frontier model, which significantly improves cybersecurity, coding, and long-horizon agentic tasks. It achieved a score of 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 and demonstrated strong performance in vulnerability finding (88.2% on SEC-bench Pro), exploitation (54.8% on ExploitGym), and patching (71.0% on CWE-bench). The model is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens and is initially being rolled out to cyber defenders and trusted partners.

Model releases Mistral Releases Weights for Trillion-Parameter ML4 AI Model

Mistral has released the weights for its trillion-parameter open-source AI model, Mistral Large 4 (ML4), nicknamed "Le Chonk," targeting cybersecurity applications. The model, available on Hugging Face and via API, offers state-of-the-art performance with 47% fewer tokens per task than competitor GLM-5.3, making it more economical. Mistral emphasizes providing sovereign cyber defense capabilities for enterprises and governments to deploy and own frontier AI.

Model releases EmbeddingGemma 2: Unified Multimodal Representations

Google has released EmbeddingGemma 2, an open multimodal embedding model that offers unified and efficient representations for text, images, audio, and video. It achieves a new Pareto frontier in comprehensive embedding benchmarks, outperforming specialist models and larger models by 2 to 12 times. The model includes simplified per-layer embeddings and a multi-stage curriculum, along with flexible design features for resource-constrained environments like selective modality encoder loading and adjustable vision token budgets.

Policy & business Ex-Palantir Execs Launch Dream with $17M for AI Construction

Ex-Palantir executives Andy Elder and Arjun Mathur have launched Dream with $17 million in seed funding from Andreessen Horowitz and others, aiming to build AI world models for the construction industry. Their startup develops "structural cognition" models trained on over 100 million square feet of building data to automate building design and engineering. Dream's platform generates structurally precise designs that account for internal elements like plumbing and HVAC, and is already deployed across 10 projects in Texas and California.

Policy & business Wabi, AI Agent Building Mini-Apps, Valued at $1.25B

Wabi, an AI agent that builds mini-apps, has raised $55 million at a $1.25 billion valuation, pivoting its focus to become a personal AI messenger. Founded by Eugenia Kuyda, previously of Replika, Wabi now aims to create interfaces for tasks like meal planning or fitness plans using its agentic capabilities. This funding, led by IVP with participation from a16z and Amazon Alexa Fund, reflects investor confidence in the booming AI agent market.

Policy & business Details of 41,000+ US immigration agents leaked

Details of over 41,000 U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) personnel, including immigration enforcement agents, have been leaked online. The breach, claimed by 'Department of Peace' and shared by 'Distributed Denial of Secrets', includes names, work addresses, phone numbers, and operational details. The DHS is actively investigating the incident, which follows other recent government agency hacks.

Policy & business Microsoft profits soar to $36.6bn after layoffs, AI focus

Microsoft reported soaring profits of over $36.6 billion for the quarter ending September, just days after announcing thousands of layoffs. Revenue increased by 19% to $92.3 billion, driven by a 30% rise in its Cloud business. Despite significant investments in AI, CEO Satya Nadella highlighted AI development's role in driving technology adoption and real-world impact.
