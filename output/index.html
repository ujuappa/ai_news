Tenstorrent Hits $6.2B Valuation to Scale Open-Weight AI Chips

Tenstorrent, a chip startup, has raised $1.5 billion, pushing its valuation to $6.2 billion, up from $2.6 billion in late 2024. The funding, led by Trace Capital and Innovation X, will be used to scale chips tailored for open-weight AI models and video generation. This comes amidst reports of acquisition interest from Intel and Qualcomm and highlights increasing competition in the AI hardware market.

Policy & business US Bans China's DeepSeek AI from Government Use

The US Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has banned all AI technology from Chinese firm DeepSeek within civilian government agencies and any technology built using its models. The ban, which took effect in late July, follows White House criticism of Chinese AI firms and claims by US companies like Anthropic and OpenAI of their models being copied. DeepSeek, known for its open-source and cost-effective models, has gained significant popularity globally, with its training costing only $6 million compared to GPT-4's $100 million.

Model releases Nvidia Launches New Open Models for Enterprise, PCs

Nvidia launched new open-weight AI models: the 120-billion-parameter Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for enterprise agents, and lightweight Bolt models for AI PCs. Super VL claims similar accuracy to larger models and faster task completion than competitors like Anthropic and OpenAI for agentic tasks. These models aim to support both enterprise and consumer AI agents, offering organizations more control over their AI deployments.

Policy & business Musk, Anthropic Pledge Public Share Giveaways

Elon Musk's SpaceX and AI giant Anthropic have pledged major financial giveaways, with Musk promising one SpaceX share to every American child next year and Anthropic committing 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund. These pledges come amid growing scrutiny over AI's impact and as both companies prepare for potential blockbuster stock market listings. Anthropic's fund could receive an additional 2% of shares for every 50% increase in valuation and will not be used for lobbying.

Policy & business Anthropic Commits 5% of Shares to Public Benefit Fund

Anthropic is committing 5% of its shares to a new Public Benefit Fund, with an additional 2% to be added for every 50% increase in the company's value, subject to stockholder approval. This initiative aims to align the company's growth with its public benefit mission. This fund is structured to increase its stake in the company as Anthropic's value grows.

Policy & business Hackers Hit AI Coding Tool OpenCode, Users Face Charges

Hackers attacked OpenCode, an AI coding tool, leading to unauthorized charges on 1.4% of its Console users' AI accounts. The incident, framed as a "large-scale distillation attack," exposed API keys for services like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google AI. This event highlights challenges for companies without vetted access to AI models in defending against sophisticated, AI-driven cyberattacks, and raised questions about model safety filters hindering defensive actions.

Policy & business Tolan Secures $30M for AI Companion, Valuation Rises

Tolan, an AI companion startup, has secured an additional $30 million in funding from investors including Union Square Ventures and Khosla Ventures, doubling its total raised capital. Tolan reports over 250,000 active users and double-digit millions in annual recurring revenue.

Policy & business Ex-OpenAI Worker Had Saudi Support in Device Planting

A former OpenAI worker, Abdulrahman Alfozan, is accused of secretly planting a recording device in an OpenAI meeting room, with court filings alleging "considerable support" from the Saudi government. Prosecutors claim Alfozan was in frequent communication with a Saudi royal family member and had a one-way flight to Saudi Arabia paid for by a military official for his wife and son after his arrest. The incident is considered a national security risk, as government officials met in the room where the device was found.
