Claude to Start Watermarking AI-Generated Text
Anthropic's Claude AI will begin watermarking AI-generated text to comply with EU regulations, which will involve changing how the chatbot makes small, random choices in its output. While intended for transparency and accountability, concerns are raised that this watermarking process could potentially degrade the quality or naturalness of Claude's generated text. The initiative reflects a broader industry trend toward implementing mechanisms to identify AI-created content.

DFM Mimir v1: 1B Parameter HRM Model with Permissible Data
DFM Mimir v1 is a 1-billion-parameter language model built on the Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM) architecture, trained entirely on permissible post-training data. It achieves competitive performance for English and a new state-of-the-art for Danish, outperforming the original HRM-Text 1B and competing with larger frontier models like Qwen 3.5 4B and Gemma 4 E2B across 20 benchmarks. The model is available on the Hugging Face Hub.
