OpenAI Launches GPT-6.1 Sol, Matches Astra, Costs Less
OpenAI has launched GPT-6.1 Sol, which they claim nearly matches GPT-6 Astra's intelligence for agentic coding and professional tasks, but at one-fifth the token price. The launch comes after GPT-6 Astra was reportedly scrapped due to safety concerns. GPT-6.1 Sol offers significant improvements in complex tasks, programming, document understanding, and multi-step workflows, with reduced factual errors and enhanced safety measures.
