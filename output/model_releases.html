Gemini 4 Carbon: Google's next frontier model
Google has announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its latest frontier model, which significantly improves cybersecurity, coding, and long-horizon agentic tasks. It achieved a score of 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 and demonstrated strong performance in vulnerability finding (88.2% on SEC-bench Pro), exploitation (54.8% on ExploitGym), and patching (71.0% on CWE-bench). The model is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens and is initially being rolled out to cyber defenders and trusted partners.

Mistral Releases Weights for Trillion-Parameter ML4 AI Model
Mistral has released the weights for its trillion-parameter open-source AI model, Mistral Large 4 (ML4), nicknamed "Le Chonk," targeting cybersecurity applications. The model, available on Hugging Face and via API, offers state-of-the-art performance with 47% fewer tokens per task than competitor GLM-5.3, making it more economical. Mistral emphasizes providing sovereign cyber defense capabilities for enterprises and governments to deploy and own frontier AI.
