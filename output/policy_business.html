Nvidia Invests $1.5B in OpenAI Data Center, $105B Credit
Nvidia is investing $1.5 billion in SB Energy, a data center developer linked to SoftBank and OpenAI, and will provide up to $105 billion in credit. This investment secures Nvidia as the sole compute infrastructure supplier for OpenAI's Ports-Pike data center, which could scale up to 8 gigawatts. The project includes building a $33 billion natural gas power plant, highlighting the immense infrastructure costs and energy demands of large-scale AI operations.
