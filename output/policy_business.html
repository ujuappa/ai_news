The company is actively working to secure launch capacity, particularly with SpaceX's Starship, as the market for rocket transportation tightens up.

The company, which provides data labeling services, retains 60% to 70% of this revenue. Micro1 also emphasizes that it does not sell its data to Chinese model makers, differentiating itself from some competitors amidst industry controversy.
