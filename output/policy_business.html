ChatGPT Health Adds Epic Integration for Clinicians
OpenAI's ChatGPT Health is integrating with Epic's electronic health record (EHR) system, allowing clinicians to import patient data and use AI for querying. This integration provides read-only access to health records for tasks like summarizing notes and reviewing patient history, but AI will not write back to records. OpenAI also introduced a Healthcare Public Data plugin and enables compliant workflows for organizations with a Business Associate Agreement, while emphasizing that AI is not for diagnosis or treatment, following prior legal issues regarding unsafe recommendations.

Empirik Launches with $21M to Predict Outages
Sequoia-incubated Empirik has spun out as an independent company after raising $21 million in seed funding from Sequoia, Canapi, and Alumni Ventures. Empirik aims to predict and prevent tech outages using AI by tracking system changes and inferring their ripple effects across infrastructure. The startup claims to be a new type of observability tool, already serving Fortune 500 customers and automating routine troubleshooting for DevOps teams.
