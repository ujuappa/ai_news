Databricks Raises $5B at $190B Valuation
Databricks secured a $5 billion funding round at a $190 billion valuation, after initially aiming for $1 billion but facing $15 billion in investor interest. CEO Ali Ghodsi stated the company's annualized run rate revenue hit $7 billion, growing 80%, with its core cloud data warehouse at $1.5 billion, growing 100% year-over-year. The capital is needed for significant cloud commitments and expensive AI research, despite having already raised $20 billion in the past 20 months.

Nvidia's $500B Plan to Fund AI Data Centers, Support Aging GPUs
Nvidia has secured commitments of up to $500 billion from major financial firms to build AI data centers, but the more significant aspect is its plan to guarantee a portion of the value of aging GPUs used as collateral. Nvidia will cover up to 25% of the difference if these chips don't retain their expected value, mitigating risk for lenders and fostering a secondary market for used hardware. This innovative, yet risky, financial strategy aims to ensure continued demand and funding for Nvidia's AI infrastructure, distinct from past cautionary tales like Lucent Technologies.
