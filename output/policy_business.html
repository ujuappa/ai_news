Texas Halts New Data Centers, Governor Calls Audits
Texas Governor Greg Abbott has halted new data center projects and mandated audits by the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT) and ERCOT, the state's grid operator. This move comes as interconnection requests for data centers and crypto mining facilities have swelled to 474 gigawatts, more than five times ERCOT's peak demand, raising concerns about overwhelming the state's power grid. The audits will collect detailed information on electricity and water demand, noise, light controls, and tax incentives.
