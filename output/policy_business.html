Binance Hit by $6.3 Billion Hack, North Korea Suspected
Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, reported that $6.3 billion in digital assets were impacted by what is considered the largest crypto hack on record. While about half of the stolen assets have been frozen, many remain with the hackers, and Binance has pledged to cover all losses. Cybersecurity firms, including Check Point and Chainalysis, attribute the incident to North Korea's TraderTraitor (Lazarus Group), known for targeting cryptocurrency companies to fund weapons programs.
