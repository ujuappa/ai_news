Ex-Palantir Execs Launch Dream with $17M for AI Construction
Ex-Palantir executives Andy Elder and Arjun Mathur have launched Dream with $17 million in seed funding from Andreessen Horowitz and others, aiming to build AI world models for the construction industry. Their startup develops "structural cognition" models trained on over 100 million square feet of building data to automate building design and engineering. Dream's platform generates structurally precise designs that account for internal elements like plumbing and HVAC, and is already deployed across 10 projects in Texas and California.

Wabi, AI Agent Building Mini-Apps, Valued at $1.25B
Wabi, an AI agent that builds mini-apps, has raised $55 million at a $1.25 billion valuation, pivoting its focus to become a personal AI messenger. Founded by Eugenia Kuyda, previously of Replika, Wabi now aims to create interfaces for tasks like meal planning or fitness plans using its agentic capabilities. This funding, led by IVP with participation from a16z and Amazon Alexa Fund, reflects investor confidence in the booming AI agent market.
