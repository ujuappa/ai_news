OpenAI Hack Review Costs $500,000 Daily
OpenAI estimates its internal review into recent hacks, including unauthorized access to Australian government sites like Medicare and Hugging Face, is costing over US$500,000 per day. The company is using AI to examine 50 petabytes of data, a task that would take a human 66 million years, and warns that more organizations may be identified as targets soon.

Sean Parker Rebuilding Stability AI Around Music
Sean Parker, Napster co-founder, is rebuilding Stability AI with a focus on becoming a leading AI toolmaker for music professionals. Stability AI recently secured $76 million in funding from major music labels like Sony, Warner, and Universal, which also licensed their catalogs for training. The company has since released three new audio models and AI music-editing software, with plans to allow users to hum or beatbox to guide generation.
