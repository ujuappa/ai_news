The global demand for power, projected to double by 2030 for data centers, has exhausted the capacity of the few companies capable of producing these specialized, heat-resistant blades.

Anthropic stated it disagrees with the claims and intends to defend itself robustly, following previous similar IP lawsuits against the company.
