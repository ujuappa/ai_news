AstraZeneca Partners With insitro AI in $1.5 Billion Deal
AstraZeneca has partnered with insitro in a deal potentially worth up to $1.5 billion, including a $40 million upfront payment. The collaboration aims to use insitro's AI-driven "Virtual Human platform" for target identification and therapeutic design in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) and cardiomyopathies. AstraZeneca will handle clinical development, regulatory work, and commercialization, while insitro will receive royalties on global sales.
