DeepSeek Reportedly Raises $15B After US Restrictions
DeepSeek has reportedly raised $15 billion, or 100 billion yuan, just days after the US imposed restrictions on its AI technology. This massive funding round, potentially from investors like CATL and Tencent, is one of China's largest and follows earlier rounds in June and July valuing the company at $50 billion and $71 billion respectively. The Hangzhou-based AI startup is increasing spending on AI infrastructure and open models for both domestic and international markets, despite US restrictions, and plans a potential IPO in China as early as next year.

PHP Armageddon: OpenAI, Google's Wiz Find Ancient Holes
The NSA warned of two critical, decades-old flaws in PHP, the programming language powering 70% of websites, found by AI models like OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and Google's Wiz Atlas and Gemini 4 Argon. Patches have been issued, with the NSA recommending taking unpatched sites offline due to the severity of the vulnerabilities. While AI helped discover these previously hard-to-exploit flaws, it also makes them easier for hackers to adapt, creating a race for websites to update to PHP 8.5.12 before exploits emerge.
