California No Robo Bosses Act bans AI-only worker firings
California has enacted the "No Robo Bosses Act of 2026," becoming the first state to restrict AI's role in employee termination and disciplinary decisions. The law, SB 947, mandates human corroboration with additional information when AI is the primary basis for such decisions and requires employers to inform affected workers about AI involvement and provide a human contact. This aims to prevent AI errors and bias, ensuring human oversight in critical employment judgments.
