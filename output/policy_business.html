China Builds Huge Data Centers for AI Ambitions
China is rapidly building massive data centers in Inner Mongolia, demonstrating "China speed" in infrastructure development, to power its national AI ambitions. These facilities are critical for China's goal of embedding AI across 90% of industries and society by 2030, a key aspect of its competition with the US. The country is also fostering a strong talent pool, with more engineers returning from the US due to tensions and immigration restrictions, enabling a robust domestic AI ecosystem.

Snorkel AI Triples Valuation to $3.5B with $350M Series E
Snorkel AI has raised $350 million in Series E funding at a $3.5 billion valuation, nearly tripling its previous valuation of $1.3 billion. The company, which provides data-as-a-service including training datasets and simulated environments, now reports an annualized revenue run rate of $375 million. This growth is driven by the high demand for training data from AI labs and corporations, with Snorkel utilizing a hybrid approach of software-generated and expert-curated data.
