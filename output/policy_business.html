Legality of AI Training on Copyrighted Books: It’s Complex

A recent ruling against Anthropic, requiring a $1.5 billion settlement, clarified that while AI training itself was lawful, pirating books from illegal sources was not. Legal experts suggest this ruling might favor AI companies by distinguishing between "reading" and "copying" copyrighted material.
