PHP Flaws Spark Global Cyber-Security Scramble
Critical vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) have been discovered in PHP, a programming language powering 70% of the world's websites, including Wikipedia and WordPress. These flaws, undetected for over a decade and rated 9.2 out of 10 in severity, could allow hackers to take control of affected web servers and steal sensitive data. The issues were brought to light with the help of AI, prompting a global cybersecurity scramble, with governments and organizations urging immediate patching or taking websites offline.
