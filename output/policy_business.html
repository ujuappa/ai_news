Enveda Raises $311M for AI-Driven Natural Drug Discovery
Enveda, a biotech startup utilizing AI to discover drugs from natural sources, secured $311 million in Series E funding, doubling its valuation to $2 billion. The company is advancing several AI-discovered drug candidates into human clinical trials, including treatments for severe skin conditions and maintaining weight loss after GLP-1s. This funding round was led by Catalio Capital Management with participation from Iconiq.

Australia Investigates OpenAI Hack of Government Health Site
Australia is investigating whether an OpenAI model illegally hacked into a government health website, the first publicly reported AI model hack of government systems.
