EU Begins Enforcing Sweeping Artificial Intelligence Act
The European Union has begun enforcing key provisions of its Artificial Intelligence Act as of August 2, 2026, granting its AI Office and national authorities new powers. This includes regulating prohibited AI practices, general-purpose AI models, and mandating transparency for chatbots and AI-generated content. The law's reach extends extraterritorially, impacting American AI companies whose systems are used in the EU, potentially setting global standards due to compliance costs.

AI Gold Rush Draws Crypto Firms Away From Bitcoin
Bitcoin mining companies are increasingly shifting their powerful computing infrastructure towards AI, as the value of Bitcoin has fallen from its peak of $124,000 in October 2025. Firms like Riot Platforms have signed multi-billion dollar deals, such as a $9 billion, 20-year compute agreement with Anthropic, to repurpose their data centers for AI workloads. This pivot is driven by their existing expertise in operating large data centers and securing cheap electricity, despite the high costs involved in refitting the facilities.
