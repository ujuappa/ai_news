Anthropic CEO Calls for AI Development Slowdown
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei advocates for slowing down the pace of AI development and increasing monitoring due to serious risks, proposing a three-point plan including independent evaluation, industry-wide, and global regulation. His call comes amid increasing warnings from Anthropic's own researchers, some of whom believe there's a greater than 10% chance AI could lead to human extinction within a decade. Amodei commits Anthropic to this unilateral slowdown and urges other frontier AI companies to match it.
