SpaceX shares plunge amid tech, AI stock fall
Shares in Elon Musk's SpaceX and other tech and AI stocks plummeted following the Democrats' midterm election victory, with the Nasdaq Composite falling over 5% at one point. The market reacted to fears of tougher scrutiny on data center construction and the AI industry, especially in states where Democrats won on anti-data center platforms. OpenAI's Greg Brockman and Meta's Dina Powell McCormick acknowledged the industry needs to improve communication with communities about AI's benefits amidst this backlash.
