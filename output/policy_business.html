Google's €13bn Finland investment for AI infrastructure
Google is making its largest single investment in Europe, committing €13 billion to Finland for AI infrastructure, including three new data centers and the expansion of an existing one. As part of the deal, Google will purchase up to 50% of the electricity generated by Finland's Loviisa nuclear power plant for 22 years.
