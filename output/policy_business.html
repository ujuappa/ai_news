White House Blocks Microsoft from Foreign Worker Hiring Program
The White House has blocked Microsoft and several other major tech firms, including Adobe, Cognizant, and Infosys, from a key foreign worker hiring program. Vice President JD Vance accused these companies of misusing the H1B visa program and the Permanent Labor Certification Program (PERM), alleging they replace American workers with "foreign indentured servants." The US government will not process new or pending PERM applications from these companies, although Microsoft stated that the vast majority of its US employees are Americans and 80% of its H1B filings are for existing staff.

AI Leaderboard Arena Nears $3.1B Valuation
AI leaderboard Arena has nearly doubled its valuation to $3.1 billion in 10 months after raising a $200 million Series B round. The company, which originated as a UC Berkeley research project, reached $100 million in annualized run-rate revenue in June by crowdsourcing AI model rankings and offering commercial AI evaluation services. Arena also introduced a new alignment leaderboard to rank models on issues like unauthorized actions and deceptive completions.
