Seattle Times, Newsday Sue OpenAI and Microsoft
The Seattle Times and Newsday are suing OpenAI and Microsoft for alleged copyright infringement, claiming their journalism was used to train AI models without permission. This lawsuit follows a similar action by The New York Times in 2023, intensifying the legal battle over intellectual property rights in the age of generative AI. The plaintiffs argue that generative AI threatens to destroy the news organizations that produce its content.

OpenAI emphasizes the need for better standards in reporting unexpected AI behaviors beyond traditional security incidents.
