Discovered Materials (YC P26) – AI Agents Discover New Materials
Discovered Materials, a YC P26 startup, is developing AI agents to discover new materials for the semiconductor industry. Their focus is on addressing the increasing heat generation of GPUs, with Nvidia & AMD nearly doubling Thermal Design Power (TDP) in new chips (H100 at 700W, Blackwell at 1.2 kW, Rubin at 2.3 kW). The startup aims to reduce datacenter energy and water consumption by finding materials that improve heat dissipation.
