Confluent Introduces Streamhouse Architecture to Power AI
Confluent, an IBM company, has launched "Streamhouse," a new data architecture designed to provide AI agents with real-time business context. This architecture allows AI applications to access and act on the most current business information at machine speed, addressing issues where AI acts on outdated data from traditional data lakes or warehouses. Key to this is the general availability of Lightning Tables on AWS, enabling low-latency querying of live streaming data directly within Confluent's platform.
