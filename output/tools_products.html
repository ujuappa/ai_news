Replika Founder Eugenia Kuyda Launches Wabi AI Assistant
Eugenia Kuyda, founder of Replika, has launched Wabi, a multiplayer personal AI assistant that fulfills her original vision from 12 years ago. Wabi functions as a messenger app with multi-threaded, agentic conversations and custom app-building capabilities that automate tasks from email integration to health tracking. This aims to offer a more organized and personalized AI assistance experience compared to single-thread alternatives.

California Launches AI Skills Matching Tool for State Jobs
California Governor Newsom announced a new GenAI-assisted 'Skills Matching' tool on CalCareers to streamline state job applications. This tool allows job seekers to upload a resume, which is then analyzed to match their skills and experience with suitable state job classifications, significantly reducing the complexity of navigating the state's hiring process. The prototype, which uses the same technology as the AskCA tool, has already seen high engagement with 21,500 clicks and nearly 8,000 resume submissions.
