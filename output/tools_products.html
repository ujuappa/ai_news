Google Brings Agentic AI to Gemini, Starting With Businesses
Google is introducing agentic AI capabilities to Gemini, initially for businesses, allowing it to perform tasks beyond just answering questions. The new unified agent, accessible via Google Cloud, can plan work, use custom skills, and connect to various business systems like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack. Users can track Gemini's progress in a "tasks inbox" and choose models, including third-party options like Anthropic's Claude.

Microsoft Releases New Nvidia-Chip AI PCs, Revamped Windows 11
Microsoft has released new Nvidia-chip AI PCs, including the Surface Laptop Ultra (starting at $2,600) and the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box (starting at $6,000), running a revamped Windows 11 with "Execution Containers" for sandboxing AI agents. These devices are designed to run AI models locally on-device, leveraging Nvidia's RTX Spark chip. Microsoft is targeting developers, offering up to $1,000 off for MacBook Pro trade-ins, and Dell also announced its XPS 16 Creator Edition PC.
