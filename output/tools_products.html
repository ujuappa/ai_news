Circuit Breaker Labs Aims to Make AI Safer for Kids
Circuit Breaker Labs, a Startup Battlefield 200 finalist, is developing AI agents to make AI safer, especially for children, by mimicking diverse users to red-team models for harmful interactions. Motivated by cases of psychological harm from chatbots, their agents simulate varied speech patterns, slang, and cultural nuances to detect and prevent dangerous responses.
