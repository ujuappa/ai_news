ChatGPT Can Now Send Texts via Apple Messages Plug-in
ChatGPT now offers a new Apple Messages plug-in, allowing users to connect their Messages inbox directly with the chatbot. This feature enables ChatGPT to sort, analyze, edit, draft, and send messages on behalf of the user, as well as search message history. OpenAI states the plug-in runs locally to address privacy concerns, but users are encouraged to monitor ChatGPT's actions, especially regarding sending messages.

Google Offers Publishers New Way to Fight AI Traffic Losses
Google has introduced a new 'Preferred Sources' button for publishers, allowing readers to indicate favorite content sources to be highlighted across Google Search, Discover, and Google News. This initiative aims to combat AI-driven traffic losses for publishers by giving users more control over their news feeds. Google hopes this will direct more traffic to trusted sites, with studies showing users are twice as likely to click preferred sources.
