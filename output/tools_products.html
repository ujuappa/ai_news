Reliance Jio launches JioPC to turn old computers into AI PCs
Mukesh Ambani’s Reliance Jio has expanded its JioPC cloud PC service to all internet users in India, aiming to transform aging computers into AI-ready machines. Launched in July 2025, JioPC now offers standalone subscriptions, streaming virtual PCs with up to eight virtual CPUs, 16GB RAM, and 1TB storage from Jio’s cloud for as low as ₹1,000 ($11) for two months. This strategy targets India's 65 million PC users by extending hardware lifecycles and creating a market for computing capability as a service, though traditional PC ownership is preferred in India.
