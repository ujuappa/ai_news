Miles v0.1: Production-Level Post-Training RL System
Miles v0.1 is a full-stack, production-ready system for frontier post-training, building on the design principles of `slime` to ensure verified, clean, and customizable components. It prioritizes accuracy, efficiency, reliability, and scalability for reinforcement learning (RL) at frontier scale. The report details its end-to-end system, including rollout engines on SGLang, a trainer with NVIDIA Megatron-LM and PyTorch FSDP backends, and three weight-synchronization transports, supporting full-parameter RL, LoRA RL, on-policy distillation, and supervised fine-tuning.
