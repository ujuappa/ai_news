CoreWeave Ships Forge — GPU Compute Landlord Becomes Agent
CoreWeave launched Forge, a unified development layer that integrates the AI improvement lifecycle—Run, Observe, Curate, Improve, Evaluate—directly on its GPU infrastructure. Forge consolidates existing tools like Weights & Biases and OpenPipe with new services, including Model Distillation and ARIA, a research agent. Key components like Agent Lens, Sandboxes, and Registry position CoreWeave beyond a compute supplier, offering agent execution, observability, and asset management close to the GPUs, which could significantly impact performance and switching costs.
