Claude Duet App: Anthropic's Bet on Agents for Business
Anthropic has launched Claude Duet, a new AI agent app designed for professionals and small businesses, aiming to compete with other productivity assistants. Built on the Claude Opus 5.5 model, Duet can automate and manage tasks like building daily briefings and dashboards, connecting to over 6,000 plugins in the Claude Marketplace, and making online purchases with user approval. It's available as an app on iOS, Android, and Claude.ai for Pro and Max customers in a research preview, with a focus on proactive recommendations and data privacy through a new classifier.
