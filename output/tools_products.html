Additionally, Meta AI is being updated for business owners to provide insights from connected Instagram, Facebook, and Google Workspace accounts, enabling tasks like drafting documents and analyzing campaign performance.

Binance now lets AI agents trade
Binance has launched Agent OS, a platform allowing AI agents to analyze markets and execute trades for its over 300 million users. The platform integrates Binance APIs and other services, supporting AI tools like OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude Code. Binance emphasizes user control through sub-accounts and strict permissions to manage agent activity and mitigate risks, though the exchange has limited visibility into an agent's reasoning.
