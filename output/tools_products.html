The program collaborates with leading technology providers like IBM, Microsoft, Anthropic, and OpenAI to deploy proactive "virtual patches" at the network level. This aims to neutralize vulnerabilities identified by frontier AI models, such as the 14,000 previously unknown flaws Palo Alto Networks uncovered, before attackers can exploit them.
